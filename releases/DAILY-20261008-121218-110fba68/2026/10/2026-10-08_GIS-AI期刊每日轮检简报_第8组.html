论文题名

焦点-分区统计：一种融合地理邻近和环境相似的空间统计方法

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020250346 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

空间效应（包括空间依赖性和空间异质性）广泛存在于地理学研究对象中，揭示、量化并有效应对空间效应是空间统计的核心任务。焦点统计（Focal Statistics）和分区统计（Zonal Statistics）作为两类常用的空间统计方法，分别体现基于空间邻近效应和空间环境效应对空间样本进行分类和统计的基本思路。然而，在实际空间数据中，这两类空间效应往往并存，当前尚缺乏能够统一处理双重空间效应的通用统计方法。这一不足不仅制约了对复杂空间问题的有效解析，也易导致对空间效应机制的片面理解。为此，本文提出一种融合地理邻近性与环境相似性的焦点-分区统计（Focal-Zonal Statistics）方法，并开发了配套的软件工具。该方法以空间滑动窗口为基础，同时引入环境分区图层，在每个邻域窗口内识别和获取与当前像元环境属性一致的像元集合，从而实现对邻近效应与环境效应的协同应对。方法与模型部分系统阐述了新方法的理论基础及其在同时应对两类空间效应方面的优势。案例研究进一步表明，新方法在构建相似生境单元时显著增强了植被覆盖度的一致性，从而提升了植被恢复潜力评估结果的可靠性。论证了该方法提出的必要性及其在生态评估等空间统计应用中的广泛潜力。

历史牧草地重建对揭示草原牧区人地关系演变规律、解析历史土地利用与土地覆被变化（LUCC）的气候与生态效应具有科学价值。本文采用“概念界定-进展总结-未来展望”的框架，厘清牧草地的内涵与定义，梳理了国内外中国历史牧草地重建相关进展，探讨了历史牧草地研究方向。研究表明：中国历史牧草地重建研究呈现鲜明的视角分野，国内多是基于历史文献开展历史草地重建，而国际上则聚焦表征放牧活动的历史牧草地开展重建。中国历史牧草地重建仍以定性探讨为主，定量重建只在青藏高原等典型区的近三百年。HYDE等全球历史土地利用数据集中国牧草地面积和空间格局均存在较大的不确定性，并不能真实反映中国过去几百年放牧活动的时空变化特征。过去千年中国草地数据集等历史草地重建成果可为历史牧草地重建提供数据支撑和方法借鉴。未来要利用反映放牧强度的多源指标来估算历史牧草地面积，并辨识影响放牧活动空间格局的主导因素，借助历史GIS进行空间建模，推进历史牧草地空间重建。本研究将推动中国历史LUCC重建从“耕地中心”向“农牧并重”范式转变。

论文题名

若尔盖黄河唐克段MIS 3a阶段以来野火活动及环境变化

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020250284 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

对青藏高原东部若尔盖盆地实地考察，在黄河唐克段选取了一个沉积序列完整的沉积剖面。通过粒度、磁化率和炭屑浓度的变化，分析了若尔盖盆地MIS 3a阶段以来沉积环境，揭示了野火活动与古气候、古植被之间的关系。研究结果表明：MIS 3a阶段以来，若尔盖盆地区域性野火占优势，主要以燃烧木本植物为主，而局地性野火主要以燃烧草本植物为主。其中，在30.9 ka之前的暖湿气候条件下，盆地处于古湖环境，在深湖静水状态下沉积了湖相淤泥层，此时区域性野火活动剧烈，频率较高，以燃烧木本植物为主，局地性野火以燃烧草本植物为主；18.2—14.6 ka期间，气候寒冷，强劲的流水动力作用搬运来的泥砂，在风沙改造活动和冻融作用下形成了褶皱-河漫滩风沙层，此时有限的植被生物量限制了野火活动的发生；Bølling-Allerød期间的14.6—12.5 ka，气候温暖湿润，在静水动力作用和较强的风力作用下形成了滨浅湖砂层，此时野火活动频率低；12.5—11.7 ka的新仙女木事件（YD）期间，气候寒冷干旱，黄河源区搬运来的浊黄橙砂受到风力作用和冻融作用的改造，形成了褶皱砂层，此时干燥的植物有利于野火发生，野火频率增高；11.7—4.8 ka的全新世早中期，气候整体暖湿，降雨量多，在静水动力和风化成壤改造作用下形成了泥沼土层，此时野火发生频率较高，仍以区域性野火为主，燃烧物均以木本植物为主；4.8—1.8 ka的全新世中晚期，沉积物受到较强的生物风化成壤作用形成了古土壤层，此时野火活动总体减弱，局地性野火频率升高，可能是古人类向高海拔扩张导致的；1.8 ka以来，气候较为干冷，较弱的生物风化成壤作用下形成了草甸土层，但可能受畜牧业活动影响，局地性野火频率增强。该研究结果有助于深入了解若尔盖盆地野火活动和气候演变的过程。

Although the traditional optimal configuration method for long/short-duration energy storage systems (ESSs) has been widely researched, it focuses more on system economy and cost. Furthermore, it neglects to assess the impact of energy storage (ES) support capacity on system voltage stability under different configurations, and does not consider how to maximize ES support capacity. Therefore, this paper proposes a stability-oriented optimal configuration method based on DMSCR. First, a hybrid power system model is constructed, and the dynamic multi-infeed short-circuit ratio (DMSCR) indicator is proposed to effectively and quantitatively characterize the ability of ES to support grid strength. Second, a three-tier ES optimal configuration framework is designed with the goal of improving support capacity and cost constraints in mind. It achieves the lowest economic cost and optimal configuration of ES grid connection nodes and capacity. Third, a Newton-Raphson-based iterative optimization approach is developed to address the configuration problem. On this basis, the optimal configuration of ESSs is formulated as an optimization problem with eigenvalue constraints. It can effectively suppress local optima traps while improving convergence efficiency and reducing the number of iterations. Finally, the simulation results show that the proposed stability-oriented optimal configuration method based on DMSCR is effective.

Miniature bionic curved compound eye for high-precision imaging, recognition and tracking

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3365-6 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Bionic compound eyes have emerged as a promising solution for high-precision and real-time imaging in complex scenarios, yet existing designs face challenges including poor imaging quality, low recognition efficiency, and difficulties in target tracking. To address these issues, this study proposes an ellipsoidal-ommatidia negative-meniscus-substrate compound eye (EONMSCE) tailored for planar optoelectronic detection. The EONMSCE features a honeycomb full-fill arrangement of ellipsoidal ommatidia, which optimizes imaging uniformity and enhances the brightness of edge ommatidia. High-precision fabrication of the EONMSCE was achieved via two-photon polymerization (TPP) lateral additive manufacturing. Optical performance validation using spot diagrams and modulation transfer function (MTF) analysis confirmed that the EONMSCE meets preset imaging requirements, with a measured field of view (FOV) of 82° that aligns well with the theoretical value of 82.2°. Compared to traditional spherical compound eyes, the EONMSCE increases edge ommatidia imaging brightness by up to 48%. To enable fast target acquisition and dynamic tracking, the YOLO11 visual algorithm was integrated into the EONMSCE system. Multi-variant γ lamellar evolution in γ-TiAl alloys

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3484-6 · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

The morphology of nanoscale γ lamellae strongly influences the mechanical properties of fully lamellar Ti-Al alloys; however, the coarsening mechanisms governing these nanostructures remain poorly understood. This work combines phase-field simulations and experiments to investigate the growth and coarsening of γ lamellae. It reveals multiple lamellar orientation-dependent coarsening mechanisms, including coalescence, Ostwald ripening, and internal interface migration. The simulated planar or step-like γ/γ interfaces agree well with the experimental observations. The competing effects between elastic energy and chemical driving force on the evolution kinetics of γ lamellae are quantitatively characterized. Elevated elastic energy suppresses coarsening and refines the lamellar structure, whereas a large chemical driving force accelerates precipitation, coarsening, and splitting. A four-stage kinetic sequence is identified for γ lamellar evolution: nucleation and growth, quasi-steady-state coarsening, splitting, and steady-state coarsening. These findings offer novel insights into the complex coarsening mechanisms and kinetic pathways of multi-variant γ lamellae in γ-TiAl alloys.

论文题名

Polypyrrole-mediated photothermal catalysis for nitroarenes reduction over urchin-like dual-active-sites catalyst

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-025-3388-y · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Metal sulfide-based photothermal materials have attracted significant attention for catalytic applications; however, their development faces intrinsic limitations such as rapid recombination of photogenerated charge carriers, limited photostability, and a scarcity of accessible active sites. To address these challenges, we developed a rationally designed photothermal catalyst consisting of uniformly dispersed Pd species anchored on polypyrrole (PPy) functionalized urchin-like bismuth sulfide (Bi2S3) microspheres. Crucially, the conductive and photothermal PPy layer not only mediates efficient interfacial charge transfer, significantly suppressing carrier recombination and enhancing photogenerated electron utilization, but also acts as a protective barrier, mitigating sulfur poisoning of the Pd species by isolating them from direct contact with the Bi2S3 substrate. The resultant Pd/Bi2S3@PPy photothermal catalyst possesses dual active sites (i.e., Pd and Bi2S3) and exhibits a high photothermal conversion efficiency under near-infrared (NIR) irradiation. In the reduction reaction of nitroarenes with NaBH4, the Pd/Bi2S3@PPy demonstrates high photothermal catalytic activity, favorable stability, and broad substrate applicability. This work establishes a rational design strategy for developing highly efficient and stable photothermal catalysts, offering broad potential for advancing NIR-driven chemical transformations.

Ultrahigh anisotropic mobility and strong polarization-sensitive photocurrent response in ZnO/SeMoSiP2 van der Waals heterojunction: A first-principles study for advanced photodetectors

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3347-0 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

In this study, we systematically explored the structural stability, electronic-optical properties, and photodetection potential of ZnO/SeMoSiP2 van der Waals heterojunctions via first-principles calculations. SeMoSiP2 (direct bandgap: 1.37 eV) has a 3.7% lattice mismatch with ZnO; the ZnO/Se (Se-side stacked) and ZnO/P (P-side stacked) heterojunctions are ther-modynamically, kinetically, and mechanically stable. Both are type-I direct bandgap semiconductors (1.34 eV), exhibiting interfacial charge transfer and a built-in potential difference. ZnO/Se exhibits higher carrier mobility (max electron: 405 cm2 V−1 s−1; max hole: 1450 cm2 V−1 s−1) and weak anisotropy, while ZnO/P shows remarkable electron mobility anisotropy (Y/X = 4.61). The heterojunctions have significantly enhanced visible-light absorption (max 2.9 × 105 cm−1) vs. pristine materials, with bandgaps and absorption peaks tunable by biaxial strain. Photodetection simulations show ZnO/Se (armchair direction) achieves a 16.1 a2 0/photon peak photocurrent at 517 nm (outperforming traditional semiconductors), and ZnO/P (zigzag direction) has a maximum polarization extinction ratio of 45. This work proposes a high-performance ZnO/SeMoSiP2 system, clarifies the interface-driven anisotropic photoresponse mechanism, and supports designing high-sensitivity, polarization-selective photodetectors.

Boosted in-situ self-Fenton photocatalytic purification of metronidazole on g-C3N4/CdIn2S4

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3378-9 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Designing photocatalytic materials with efficient hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) generation and pollutant purification capabilities remains a significant challenge. Herein, a novel carbon-doped g-C3N4/CdIn2S4 heterojunction (denoted C-GCN/CIS) was designed to synergistically couple visible-light-driven in-situ H2O2 generation with a Fe2+-activated self-Fenton process for efficient metronidazole (MTZ) removal. The optimized C-GCN/CIS-50 catalyst exhibited a high H2O2 production rate of 5762 µmol g−1 h−1, which, upon Fe2+ activation, generated hydroxyl radicals (·OH), achieving a 79.2% total organic carbon (TOC) reduction within 60 min. Mechanistic studies revealed that interfacial charge redistribution enhances electron transfer from CdIn2S4 (CIS) to carbon-doped g-C3N4 (C-GCN), promoting charge separation and optimizing the adsorption/desorption of key oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) intermediates. Active-site analysis identified the interfacial nitrogen atom as the primary adsorption center for end-on *OOH. Upon *OOH binding, the Bader charge on this nitrogen atom changed significantly, highlighting its key role in interfacial charge reorganization and *OOH formation. These findings establish the interfacial N atom as the central active site for selective H2O2 generation. Overall, this work introduces a novel heterojunction photocatalyst for efficient in-situ self-Fenton remediation and offers mechanistic insights into interfacial engineering and active-site regulation for advancing photocatalytic H2O2 production and Fe-ion cycling.

论文题名

A mechanical metamaterial with tunable Poisson’s ratio and modulus for non-uniform deformation

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3480-3 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Mechanical metamaterials with tunable properties have attracted considerable attention in recent years. This paper presents a novel lattice metamaterial composed of inclined beams, whose macroscopic behavior is governed by three independent angular parameters (α, β, and θ). A theoretical framework based on Euler-Bernoulli beam theory is established to predict the elastic modulus and Poisson’s ratio, and is validated through finite element simulations and quasi-static tensile experiments on 3D-printed thermoplastic polyurethane specimens. The results demonstrate three key capabilities. First, the Poisson’s ratio can be continuously tuned over an exceptionally wide range from −1.07 to 0.74, surpassing most existing designs. Second, quantitative sensitivity analysis reveals that β governs lateral deformation with negligible influence on stiffness, while α and θ play a coordinated regulatory role. This confirms significant decoupling of Poisson’s ratio and modulus. Third, by integrating unit cells with distinct Poisson’s ratios into a cylindrical configuration, we achieve spatially programmable radial expansion under axial loading, with the middle segment expanding radially and the top/bottom segments contracting. This capability offers a promising strategy for next-generation biomedical stents requiring lesion-specific dilation. This study provides a versatile design framework for lightweight, multifunctional materials requiring precise deformation control.

Poly(arylene-pyridine) membranes with adjusted aromatic structures for high temperature proton exchange membrane fuel cells

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-025-3334-2 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

High-temperature proton exchange membrane fuel cells (HT-PEMFCs) are promising candidates for efficient and clean energy conversion; however, their performance is still constrained by the trade-off between proton conductivity and mechanical stability in phosphoric acid (PA)-doped polymer electrolyte membranes. In this work, a series of poly(arylene-pyridine) copolymers were synthesized via superacid-catalyzed polyhydroxyalkylation by incorporating various fused-ring or bulky aromatic comonomers, including dibenzofuran (DBF), fluorene (Flu), 9,9-dimethylfluorene (DMF), and 9,10-diphenylanthracene (DPHA), into the poly(terphenyl acetylpyridine) (PTAP) backbone. The influence of these aromatic architectures on thermal and chemical stability, PA doping and retention, hydrogen-bonding interactions, dimensional swelling, proton conduction, and mechanical strength was systematically investigated. Among them, the DBF-containing copolymer P(TP80%-co-DBF20%) exhibited a well-balanced combination of high PA uptake (189%), low swelling, and excellent tensile strength (11.0 MPa). The DBF units not only disrupted compact polymer-chain stacking to generate additional free volume but also introduced oxygen bridges that served as multiple hydrogen-bonding sites, thereby constructing continuous proton-conducting networks. Consequently, the P(TP80%-co-DBF20%)/189%PA membrane achieved a high proton conductivity of 89.2 mS cm−1 at 180°C and delivered a peak power density of 816 mW cm−2 at 200°C under non-humidified H2/O2 conditions. Comparative analysis revealed that variations in aromatic structure directly modulate polymer chain rigidity, acid-polymer affinity, and mechanical stability, thereby determining the conductivity-mechanical property balance. These findings highlight dibenzofuran as an ideal structural segment for PTAP-based HT-PEMs and establish a versatile molecular design strategy for next-generation high-performance polymer electrolyte membranes in HT-PEMFCs.

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3360-0 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Flexible supercapacitors (FSCs), driven by the urgent demand for lightweight and multifunctional wearable electronics, have rapidly advanced owing to their simple configuration, high electrochemical stability, and power density. However, achieving high specific capacitance and stable output under extreme deformations, particularly large tensile strains, remains a critical challenge. Herein, we report an intrinsically stretchable PVA/PEDOT/CNT/PANI hybrid hydrogel (PVCPH), engineered through sequential freeze-salting-out and acid treatment to form a 3D hierarchical framework. In this architecture, the PVA backbone provides reversible macroscopic elasticity, the PEDOT/CNT network establishes a continuous mesoscopic pathway for charge transport, and proton-doped PANI nanospheres serve as highly effective microscopic pseudocapacitive contributors. Owing to this hierarchical integration, PVCPH exhibits remarkable stretchability (301.35%±5.46%) while delivering a high specific area capacitance (CA) of 2001 mF cm−2. Even in quasi-solid-state devices with gel electrolytes, the FSCs maintain a CA of 1513 mF cm−2 and achieve an outstanding energy density of 135 µWh cm−2. Furthermore, the devices preserve nearly 100% capacitance after repeated cycles of stretching, bending, and twisting, highlighting their robustness under extreme mechanical conditions. These results establish a viable strategy for designing deformation-tolerant FSCs capable of powering next-generation wearable electronics.

Whole-process control of dioxins in electric arc furnace flue gas: Progress, challenges, and prospects

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3467-0 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Against the backdrop of the accelerating green and low-carbon transformation of the steel industry, electric arc furnace (EAF) short-process steelmaking has expanded rapidly owing to its remarkable advantages in energy conservation and carbon emission reduction. However, dioxin emissions from EAF flue gas have emerged as a critical obstacle to achieving clean production in the steel industry. The major barriers to the long-term stable compliance of dioxin emissions from EAF flue gas mainly include insufficient control of chlorine-containing and organic impurities in scrap at the source, inadequate suppression of dioxin resynthesis during the flue gas cooling stage, and the limited adaptability of single end-of-pipe treatment technologies under complex and fluctuating operating conditions. Compared with relying solely on end-of-pipe treatment, establishing a whole-process collaborative control system encompassing source-stage scrap sorting and pretreatment, process optimization to improve combustion and suppress dioxin formation, and efficient end-of-pipe treatment is an important pathway for achieving ultralow dioxin emissions from EAF flue gas. Future research should prioritize the development of intelligent and precise regulation of flue gas operating conditions, synergistic multipollutant control technologies, high-efficiency and low-cost treatment materials, and highly comprehensive systems for emission standards, online monitoring, and engineering performance evaluation. The establishment of a whole-process collaborative control system will provide a solid theoretical basis and technical support for the long-term reduction of dioxin emissions, thereby facilitating the green and high-quality development of the EAF steelmaking industry.

Bispecific cell engagers: Evolving construction strategies, clinical progress, and future perspectives

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3376-1 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Bispecific cell engagers (BsCEs) have emerged as a promising class of cancer immunotherapeutics, exerting robust antitumor activity by bridging immune effector and tumor cells to trigger potent, MHC-independent tumor lysis. Bispecific antibodies (BsAbs) represent the predominant class of BsCEs currently in use, with 13 approved therapies validating their notable clinical efficacy across multiple tumor indications. However, the inherent complexity of protein engineering limits their broader clinical translation and widespread application. Persistent challenges, including heavy and light-chain mispairing, suboptimal structural stability, and enhanced costs associated with large-scale production, have driven researchers to explore alternative, more feasible construction paradigms. This paper systematically reviews the evolutionary landscape of BsCEs manufacturing technologies. First, mainstream genetic engineering strategies, encompassing the fragment assembly approach and IgG-like platforms, which currently serve as a gold standard for BsCEs development, are summarized. Subsequently, emerging interdisciplinary approaches that overcome traditional biosynthetic bottlenecks are highlighted. These include site-specific chemical conjugation for controllable modular assembly, antibody–nanoparticle conjugates that leverage the enhanced permeability and retention effect to optimize solid tumor targeting, and synthetic alternatives such as bispecific aptamers and peptides. A comprehensive analysis of these diverse approaches elucidates technical advantages by addressing key barriers to clinical translation. A forward-looking roadmap for the research and development of next-generation bispecific immunotherapeutics is outlined.

基于自组织映射神经网络的陕北黄土高原植被分区

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020250791 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

内部生境相对一致的植被分区是开展生态恢复重要依据。黄土高原作为国家生态安全屏障和区域协调发展关键区，生态治理成效显著，但大规模植被工程推进过程中，由于缺乏科学的生境适配机制，出现植被过度扩张、恢复植被与立地条件错位等新问题。制定与环境条件相匹配的现势性强、生态适应性佳的植被分区方案，成为提升植被恢复成效的关键。本文以陕北黄土高原为研究区，利用2000—2023年气象数据（≥10℃积温、1月和7月均温、干燥度、降雨量），结合DEM、土壤、植被和地貌类型等多源数据，构建三级植被分区指标体系，采用能够捕捉复杂数据内在结构的无监督聚类模型——自组织映射神经网络（Self-Organizing Feature Map, SOFM），结合GIS进行分区制图，并利用轮廓系数（Silhouette Coefficient, SC）和戴维森堡丁指数（Davies-Bouldin Index, DBI）进行交叉验证，通过多指标动态平衡策略提高分区方案的稳健性。结果表明：① 陕北黄土高原水热梯度分异显著，地貌、土壤、植被格局与水热梯度高度耦合，空间分异特征清晰；② 基于加权评分确定最佳聚类效果，将研究区划分为2个植被区域、5个植被地带、11个植被区。研究提出的多尺度分区框架，揭示了不同分区植被类型与生境因子的空间适配规律，可为区域植被恢复精准施策、生态系统优化及可持续发展提供科学依据。

论文题名

黄土区坡面水土保持措施对流域水沙关系的影响

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020251019 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

植被梯田等坡面水保措施已显著改变了黄土区景观。针对坡面水保措施是否改变流域水沙关系这一问题，选择坝库修建较少的祖厉河流域（黄丘五区）、天水站（黄丘三区）和西峰站（黄土塬区）进行了研究，研究时段从20世纪50年代至今。在天水站和西峰站，尽管流域治理减沙率高达60%左右，但仍重现了前期研究在晋西离石站（黄丘一区）的结果，即水沙关系不变、含沙量不变、减水率和减沙率接近。在祖厉河，流域治理减沙率高达82%，但60余年来日尺度水沙关系保持不变，洪水期含沙量始终为645±5% kg/m3。与天水站和西峰站不同，祖厉河的年水沙关系发生了变化，主要是由于治理后流量减幅太大，以至于小流量且低含沙量的组分显著增加。在黄土区，流量低于某一阈值后含沙量才衰减，因此只有当流域治理达到相当程度、流量减至该阈值以下时，才会诱发含沙量的降低，使得减沙效率激增，这为黄河产沙锐减提供了新的解释。研究结果表明，尽管黄土区植被和梯田建设导致“山川巨变”，但日或事件尺度的流域水沙关系保持不变。这意味着对以坡面治理为主的流域，洪水期或极端降雨下仍会出现与治理前相同的高含沙量，减沙仅通过减洪实现，因此减沙率可直接用减洪率估计，这对解决植被梯田减沙效益估算这一难题具有重要意义。

全球生产网络视角下苹果产业链概念股企业动态演变研究

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020250181 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

在全球生产分工背景下，苹果公司通过高度协同的供应链体系在中国构建了庞大的生产网络。本文基于全球生产网络（GPN）理论，以中国132家苹果产业链概念股企业（果链企业）为研究对象，揭示其动态演变过程与机制。研究发现：果链企业经历“孕育期-萌芽期-扩张期-转型期”四大发展阶段；81.8%的果链企业通过内源式驱动嵌入GPN，18.2%依赖外源式驱动。果链企业的战略耦合模式由依附式向互惠式转变，其演变机制是一种双边动态过程。在逆全球化背景下，除了领先企业通过产业转移实现“去中国化”的解耦合以外，地方果链企业也可主动通过企业转型实现“去苹果化”的解耦合与再耦合。本研究为发展中国家重构GPN权力关系提供了理论框架，同时为逆全球化下的产业链韧性治理提出策略启示。

全球天然橡胶贸易网络演化特征及驱动机制

天然橡胶是一种非遍在的战略资源，揭示全球天然橡胶贸易网络演化特征及驱动机制对维护其贸易安全具有重要意义。运用复杂网络分析、时间指数随机图模型等方法，解析1990—2022年全球天然橡胶贸易网络的演化特征，剖析其微观组织结构与驱动机制。结果表明：① 研究期内，全球天然橡胶贸易规模增长了5.42倍，泰国、科特迪瓦、越南等经济体在出口格局中的主导地位突出，马来西亚、中国、墨西哥等经济体对进口市场的影响力扩大，中国超98%的进口量高度集中于泰国和越南。② 贸易网络中的经济体与贸易联系的数量分别增加了36.46%和107.51%，网络紧密性增强，小世界现象显著，演化为泰国、马来西亚和中国“三核”结构，形成了少数出口经济体主导的4大贸易组团。③ 网络内生结构促使天然橡胶贸易网络呈现互惠式、层级式、均衡式的演化特点，且“路径依赖”特征突出。生产规模趋同、经济水平趋异是促进双边匹配的关键因素，“距离-语言-关系”的相互邻近、轮胎贸易关联促发天然橡胶贸易联系。未来，中国可通过地缘关系的促进作用，加强与非洲新兴天然橡胶出口经济体贸易合作，拓展进口来源以降低进口集中的贸易风险。

全球半导体产业贸易网络脆弱性演化研究

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020251244 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

在供需失衡、关税壁垒、技术封锁、地缘风险等多重不确定因素冲击下，全球贸易网络的脆弱性特征日益突出。本文基于2007—2023年全球半导体产业链关键产品的贸易数据，通过构建网络脆弱性评价体系，借助复杂网络分析等方法，系统分析了全球半导体产业贸易网络的脆弱性演化特征。研究结论如下：① 全球半导体产业贸易网络规模显著扩张，基础材料网络呈现主体收缩与联系强化并存特征，制造设备网络由美、日、荷等凭借技术壁垒主导，器件成品网络以东亚为核心，中国的贸易地位显著提升。② 相较2007年，2023年网络整体静态脆弱性有所降低，基础材料网络风险应对相对迟滞，且互惠性与贸易多样性不足，制造设备网络鲁棒性相对不足且层级性最为突出，器件成品网络韧性表现最优，但高度互联特性易引发风险级联效应。③ 蓄意攻击下的网络脆弱性显著高于随机攻击场景，网络抵御核心节点失效的能力整体增强，临界崩溃阈值有所提升。④ 美国各产业链环节均属于主导稳固型节点，中国逐渐从核心稳定型向主导稳固型转变；部分经济体在特定环节优势突出，多数节点抗冲击能力比较薄弱。

外部冲击下对外直接投资基础对城市产业韧性的影响

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020251341 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

在外部不确定性增加的背景下，提升城市产业韧性已成为实现产业高质量发展的关键。对外直接投资（OFDI）作为融入全球产业链和价值链的重要路径，其既有基础可能对产业韧性产生复杂影响，但现有研究对其关注较少，尤其缺乏重大外部冲击情境下的实证检验。本文基于演化论，从适应性角度界定城市产业韧性的内涵，应用Orbis数据库2014—2022年1,000余万家中国企业数据测度城市产业韧性，并以疫情这一外部冲击为研究情境，系统考察疫情前OFDI基础对疫情期间城市产业韧性的作用机制。结果表明：① 疫情前城市OFDI基础对疫情期间城市产业韧性具有显著负向作用；② 对于疫情前不相关多样化水平较高、相关多样化水平较低的城市和创新能力较弱的城市，OFDI基础对城市产业韧性的负向影响更大；③ OFDI基础的负向影响主要作用于低知识密集型产业。本研究丰富了外部冲击背景下OFDI领域的相关研究，明晰了OFDI基础对城市产业韧性的影响机制，为提升城市产业韧性提供了政策依据。

原油贸易网络的研究通常缺乏对外部事件冲击下网络近实时自组织动态调整过程和韧性特征的探讨，本文基于船舶原油贸易流量数据，构建了2019—2021年间中国原油贸易近实时网络，并运用复杂网络的韧性βeff模型和“V型”曲线，分析2020年由全球疫情导致的供应链中断场景下，中国五大港口群参与的原油贸易网络的自组织动态调整过程及其韧性特征。研究发现：① 中国原油贸易网络整体韧性处于中等水平：尽管港口群全球贸易对象港口数量从216减少至193，网络的连边数和平均度有所降低，但2021年贸易量与2019年基本持平。② 五大港口群呈现不同的韧性表征：环渤海港口群通过新增与阿曼、新加坡和科威特等国的贸易量以提高韧性应对疫情冲击；长三角港口群减少与沙特阿拉伯、伊拉克和阿曼等国的进口，转而加强与韩国、巴拿马和美国等国的合作；珠三角港口群则主要通过增加来自沙特阿拉伯、安哥拉和伊拉克等国的进口来提高韧性；东南港口群的贸易韧性较弱，至2021年底未能恢复到疫情前水平；西南港口群由于贸易量较少，未显著受到疫情冲击。③ 本研究采用的韧性βeff模型有效补充了传统网络指标对韧性的表征：环渤海港口群和长三角港口群展现出较强的网络自适应调整能力。本研究结果为中国原油贸易在突发事件冲击下的合作关系调整和航线布局优化提供了理论和实证支持，同时为保障中国原油安全提供了重要的战略指导。

产业集群作为产业分工深化与空间集聚的高级形态，是产业、区域乃至国家提升竞争位势和创新效能的重要支撑。因此，培育产业集群对于产业园区尤其是海外园区发展具有关键战略意义。本文基于关联法则理论，构建了中国海外园区产业集群形成机理的分析框架，并以中埃·泰达苏伊士经贸合作区为例，探讨其产业集群的形成要素和过程。研究发现：① 海外园区产业集群的形成，本质上是特色化定位、关联性集聚、开放式发展、主体间协同、制度化保障五大系统耦合的结果；② 演化视角下，园区产业集群的构建经历了“龙头引筑-动态聚合-系统集成”三个递次演进阶段；③ 海外园区产业集群的形成机理，可概括为五大关键要素在三阶段演进中交互耦合产生的组合效应，核心在于持续创造、增强以及优化区内各类经济关联。未来我国海外园区应精准锚定主导产业，增进内外关联交互，深化多元主体协作及制度协同，全方位培育产业集群生态。

流动人口子女就学地选择既是一个教育选择问题，也是地理选择问题，涉及社会公平、经济发展和国家未来的综合性议题。基于家庭迁移理论，研究提出了家庭劳动力配置与子女照料权衡，家庭经济能力与流入地生活成本权衡的双重分析框架，以城市等级分类为基础，使用Logit回归模型和中介效应模型分析了流动人口子女就学地选择机制和不同等级城市分异机制。研究发现流动人口子女就学地选择是在家庭双重权衡下做出的决策，省会及以上城市流动人口子女流入地就学概率显著下降，不同等级城市流动人口子女就学地选择决策的影响机制存在显著差异。县级市主要受劳动力配置与子女照料影响；普通地级市在劳动力配置与子女照料的基础上，家庭收入影响显著；省会及以上城市以房价为标志的较高生活成本和子女随迁成本产生了关键作用。由于成本约束与政策壁垒不同，家庭收入对县级市和普通地级市以直接影响为主，省会及以上城市存在“家庭年收入→主观教育投入→子女流入地就学”的完全中介传导机制。

期刊：地理研究 · DOI：10.11821/dlyj020250453 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

教育是关系人民生活水平的重要内容。教育资源的空间公平性是衡量新时期中国区域协调发展的重要指标，其中基础教育处于优先位置。本文在构建“3要素（人-财-物）+3维度（时间-空间-学段）”理论框架基础上，运用基尼系数、熵权TOPSIS及耦合协调度模型，以新疆为案例区，对2000—2020年区域基础教育资源空间差异及其与经济发展的协调性开展多尺度分析。结果显示：① 在时间维度上，基础教育资源空间差异呈现三阶段特征，表现为2000—2005年义务教育经费标准不统一致差异扩大，2005—2015年政策干预促收敛，2015年后南北疆分化致差异再扩大；② 在空间维度上，基础教育资源分布呈现降尺度差异增大的特征，县级差异系数（0.289）>地级（0.253）>自治区（省）级（0.205），生均一般公共预算教育事业费（2010年前）与万人拥有学校数（2010年后）交替成为关键指标；③ 在教育学段维度上，基础教育资源空间差异逐级递减，差异系数表现为小学（0.318）>初中（0.245）>高中（0.215）。④ 区域经济差距和城乡收入差距得到显著缩小的同时，基础教育资源的差异维持在相对稳定的低值区，且略有扩大。高、中、低经济组的基尼系数在0.21以下，近5年增幅8%~13%。⑤ 基础教育资源与经济发展水平的协调性呈现时空分异特征，全疆协调度整体提升，极度失调地州清零，南北疆发展不均衡。北疆6地州达到了高度协调（D>0.6），克拉玛依持续保持极度协调（D>0.8），南疆喀什、和田仍于处低度协调（D<0.4）。最后，文章对基础教育资源空间非公平性及其与区域经济发展水平不协调的原因进行讨论，提出了优化新疆教育资源公平配置、提升与区域经济发展水平的耦合协调水平的对策建议。

Self-organizing fuzzy tracking control for nonlinear systems with unknown multisource disturbances and its application

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-026-3398-2 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Unknown multisource disturbances always exist in nonlinear systems, especially in practical processes, which can enhance the adverse effect on the accuracy and stability of tracking control performance. To deal with this issue, a self-organizing fuzzy tracking control (SFTC) is developed to improve robustness. First, a self-organizing fuzzy neural network (SOFNN), based on the static and dynamic characteristics of neurons, is designed to approximate unknown terms of nonlinear systems. Then, the proposed model can comprehensively describe the system characteristics to assist in the tracking control. Second, a dynamic surface control (DSC) is integrated to avoid the differential explosion problem in traditional backstepping control, enabling high precision and fast trajectory tracking. Third, a Lyapunov function with tracking error, filtering error and estimation error (i.e., weight, center, and width of SOFNN parameters) is constructed to rigorously prove the stability of SFTC. It can be concluded from the theoretical proof that all closed-loop signals are semi-globally uniformly ultimately bounded (SG-UUB), and the tracking error converges to a small bounded region around zero. Finally, several simulation experiments and a real application of SFTC are conducted to validate its feasibility and effectiveness.

Due to the risk of PCM leakage and poor cyclic stability, the utilization of PCMs in industry is severely limited. Generally, porous materials are used for PCM encapsulation to prevent leakage. However, due to the poor interface compatibility between porous materials and PCMs, the composite materials suffer from incomplete PCM injection and low enthalpy value. This study used polydopamine (PDA)-modified melamine foam (MF) to improve the encapsulation of polyethylene glycol (PEG) to reveal the effect of molecular modification on the thermal performance of the composite PCMs. The results indicate that with increasing PDA modification time, the adsorption capacity of MF for PEG gradually improves, the leakage of MF-based PCMs is alleviated, and the enthalpy value increases. This study reveals that molecular modification is useful in improving the interface compatibility and heat storage capacity of the porous-based phase change composites. It fills the gap in the analysis of the influence pattern and mechanism of molecular modification on the change of phase transition enthalpy.

Deformation and breakage of carbonate sands: Coupled effects of particle size, initial shear stress, and loading stress path

期刊：Science China Technological Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11431-025-3358-0 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Carbonate sand is prone to substantial deformation and particle breakage in marine and geotechnical engineering. Specifically, larger particles are generally more susceptible to crushing. Nevertheless, it remains unclear how these responses evolve as the loading stress path rotates from 0° to 90°. Furthermore, it becomes more complex when the specimens are prepared with different particle sizes or subjected to initial shear stress states. Addressing the above issues is experimentally challenging as the conditions mentioned above are complex, but studying the above issues is necessary as such conditions exist in the field. In this study, we investigated the coupled effects of particle size, loading stress path, and initial shear stress on the shear and breakage behaviors of carbonate sands (CS) through stress-controlled drained triaxial tests. For a given loading stress path, both deformation and particle breakage increase with particle size. Meanwhile, for a given particle size, the deformation mode transitions from a contraction-dominated mode to a shear-dominated mode as the loading stress path rotates counterclockwise from 0° to 90°. The particle breakage index remains nearly constant for loading path angles below 90° under isotropic conditions, and increases markedly at 90°. Under anisotropic consolidation, the particle breakage increases obviously from a loading path angle of 71.6° to 90°. Despite these distinct deformation and breakage patterns, a unique empirical equation was proposed to describe the relationship between particle breakage and soil deformation. These findings advance the understanding of breakage and deformation behaviors under different loading stress paths and provide practical guidance for the design of marine infrastructure on carbonate sand foundations.
