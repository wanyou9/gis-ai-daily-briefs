论文题名

A threshold-calibrated machine learning framework for evaluating fault-specific seismic hazard potentials

期刊：Earth Science Informatics · DOI：10.1007/s12145-026-02265-w · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Earthquakes represent one of the most destructive natural hazards affecting human environments. Accurate estimation of seismic hazards remains a critical challenge for disaster risk mitigation. In this study, a data-driven machine learning framework was developed to evaluate regional seismic hazard potentials along the North Anatolian Fault Zone. Nine predefined fault segments were analytically investigated to forecast major destructive events (M_W ≥ 5.6). A dataset comprising 539 seismic events was utilized to construct the predictive framework. Short-to-medium-term seismic memory indicators were identified as the most critical predictors. The Extreme Gradient Boosting algorithm demonstrated superior predictive capability, yielding a final test Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve score of 0.8930. A high-resolution probability calibration was conducted with a strict “Zero Miss” operational strategy. The optimal decision boundary was formally established at a 35% probability threshold. At this specific calibration, the framework successfully captured 100% of the major earthquakes, achieving a balanced accuracy of 87.3%. Finally, the calibrated framework was deployed to evaluate future hazard probabilities across the predefined segments for their upcoming statistical cycles. This dynamic predictive tool has the potential to provide quantitative guidance for disaster management authorities to optimize logistical resource allocation and regional preparedness strategies.

论文题名

Spatio-temporal graph foundation models for earth system intelligence: an end-to-end, continual, and multimodal learning framework

期刊：Earth Science Informatics · DOI：10.1007/s12145-026-02264-x · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Spatio-temporal graph neural networks are commonly designed for a single task, a fixed or externally specified topology, and one dominant data modality. These assumptions are restrictive for Earth observation and Earth-system applications, where relationships among geographic entities change over time, observations arrive irregularly, and satellite imagery, environmental measurements, geospatial metadata, and text provide complementary evidence. This conceptual study formulates a Spatio-Temporal Graph Foundation Model (STG-FM) as an implementable framework for learning from such evolving multimodal graphs. The architecture couple’s dynamic adjacency estimation, graph-masked spatial attention, causal temporal attention, masked multimodal fusion, and continual adaptation within a unified optimization objective. Its reference specification defines encoder depth and dimensions, edge prediction, missing-modality handling, explicit spatial, temporal, structural, replay, and cross-modal losses, exponential-moving-average loss normalization, auxiliary-loss warm-up, and projected conflicting gradients. To support continuous Earth observation streams without predefined task boundaries, the replay memory organizes compressed historical subgraphs by season, climate regime, and geographic region, then combines coverage-based selection with loss-aware sampling. The scalability analysis separates candidate-edge scoring from sparse graph storage and shows that top-k sparsification reduces adjacency memory from quadratic to linear dependence on the retained neighborhood size, although exhaustive edge scoring remains a computational bottleneck. A graph-to-language adapter is also specified as a future interface for grounded explanation and decision support; causal conclusions are not claimed without interventional, counterfactual, or physics-based supervision. The paper does not report empirical performance. It contributes a reproducible mathematical specification, analytical complexity bounds, and a benchmark roadmap for testing transfer, continual retention, multimodal alignment, graph quality, and computational cost across remote-sensing, climate, and urban datasets.

论文题名

Optimization-driven hybrid XGBoost frameworks for predicting groundwater arsenic contamination in Bihar, India

期刊：Earth Science Informatics · DOI：10.1007/s12145-026-02262-z · 摘要来源：已核验来源

An outbreak of groundwater arsenic (As) contamination is a serious environmental and human health threat in Bihar, India, where groundwater is the primary source of daily water consumption by millions of people. Chronic arsenic exposure causes severe health effects which include cancer, heart diseases and neurological impairment and as such there is an immediate need to accurately predict and spatially risk assess. This study aims to novel and sound, data-intensive models that can predict the concentration of arsenic in groundwater through developed hybrid machine learning models. Two hybrid models, XGB-MPA (Extreme Gradient Boosting optimized by the Marine Predators Algorithm) and XGB-OOA (Extreme Gradient Boosting optimized by the Osprey Optimization Algorithm) were designed and used to major hydrogeochemical parameters of pH, electrical conductivity, total dissolved solids, and depth. To determine model performance, various statistical values (R², RMSE, MAE, VAF, RSR) were considered and confirmed with k-fold cross-validation, Wilcoxon signed-rank test, and Anderson-Darling probability analysis to promote strength and reliability. Findings indicated that the XGB-MPA model was better than the XGB-OOA with greater predictive accuracy (R² = 0.9752 − 0.9705, normalized RMSE = 0.0370–0.0325 ) and generalization capacity. Complementary indices (a20 = 84.29% for training and 76.67% for testing, IOA > 0.98, IOS < 0.19) further supported the predictive consistency of the XGB-MPA model within the available dataset. The proposed hybrid frameworks provide a data-driven predictive screening approach for estimating groundwater arsenic concentrations from the available hydrochemical and depth-related variables. The results demonstrate the potential of coupling XGBoost with bio-inspired optimization for arsenic prediction within the investigated dataset, while broader transferability requires validation using independent datasets incorporating additional hydrogeochemical variables. The framework may serve as a preliminary screening aid for identifying locations requiring further groundwater-quality investigation; however, operational water-management and health-related decisions should incorporate direct water-quality measurements and relevant hydrogeochemical indicators, including Fe and Mn.

论文题名

Uncertainty aware machine learning for pore pressure prediction in carbonate reservoirs

期刊：Earth Science Informatics · DOI：10.1007/s12145-026-02261-0 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Pore pressure is a first-order control on drilling safety and on the integrity of subsurface traps, yet it is rarely measured directly and must be inferred from logs. Reliable pore-pressure prediction remains challenging in carbonate reservoirs where sonic logs are unavailable and pressure measurements are sparse. This study builds a pore-pressure workflow that does not need a sonic log at any stage. The reference pressure is taken from resistivity, and the models are trained on the density, porosity and gamma curves that almost every well already has. Each prediction is issued with a 90% interval and with SHAP attribution showing which logs produced it, so the output can be used in a Jurassic carbonate of the Kohat Plateau, Pakistan, without the sonic that conventional transforms assume. The fracture gradient was then estimated using Eaton’s fracture-pressure formulation to define the drilling window. Four machine-learning models (LightGBM, CatBoost, NGBoost and a GPR) were trained using conventional wireline logs. Models were trained on a 60/20/20 split, with the validation partition used for early stopping and all reported metrics computed on the held-out test set. Uncertainty was expressed as 90% prediction intervals and checked empirically against that test set: the Gaussian process reached 94.3% coverage, close to nominal, while the two gradient-boosting models covered only about 66% of test points and are therefore over-confident at the stated level. The Gaussian process achieved the best test performance (R² = 0.94, RMSE = 586 psi, MAPE 5.5%), with CatBoost and LightGBM close behind at R² of 0.91 and 0.90. Overburden stress and bulk density emerged as the dominant predictors, consistent with effective-stress theory. The resulting profiles define a mud-weight window of about 1.05–1.6 g/cc that narrows where pore pressure is elevated, and the uncertainty band propagates into its lower edge, so that the operative lower limit becomes the upper edge of the pore-pressure interval rather than its central estimate. Which is the practical difference between a deterministic and an uncertainty-aware pressure prediction.

论文题名

GEDIMetrics: a QGIS plugin for accessing and integrating multi-product GEDI spaceborne LiDAR data

期刊：Earth Science Informatics · DOI：10.1007/s12145-026-02254-z · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

NASA’s Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation (GEDI) provides near-global, high-resolution 3D LiDAR observations of forest vertical structure. However, GEDI products are distributed as independent HDF5 files organised by beam, requiring ad-hoc workflows for data discovery, quality filtering, spatial subsetting, and footprint-level alignment across products. Existing tools either target a single product, require programming expertise, or operate outside common GIS environments. Here, GEDIMetrics is presented as an open-source QGIS plugin that integrates the full GEDI processing chain for four products focused on forest vertical structure: canopy height (L2A), vertical canopy structure (L2B), aboveground biomass density (L4A), and waveform structural complexity (L4C). The plugin implements a five-stage pipeline: granule discovery via the NASA CMR API, authenticated download, variable extraction with quality filtering, spatial subsetting, and multi-product merging using the shot number identifier, with export to GeoPackage and GeoParquet. Its core contribution is the automated footprint-level fusion of these products into a single analysis-ready dataset through a graphical interface, without scripting. GEDIMetrics was tested across two contrasting forest ecosystems (~ 100 km² each): a temperate mixed forest in northeastern France and a humid tropical rainforest in Madre de Dios, Peru. The plugin is freely available through the QGIS Plugin Repository and on GitHub.

论文题名

Assessing the relative importance of machine learning modeling components for precipitation prediction using ANOVA

期刊：Earth Science Informatics · DOI：10.1007/s12145-026-02260-1 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Accurate precipitation prediction is important for effective water resources management, yet the relative importance of different modeling decisions in machine-learning-based precipitation prediction remains insufficiently understood, particularly across basins with contrasting climatic conditions. This study systematically evaluates the relative importance of four key modeling components: outlier detection, missing-value imputation, machine learning model selection, and training data length. Monthly precipitation data from the ERA5 reanalysis product for 1990–2023 were analyzed across five Iranian basins representing different precipitation regimes. After applying eight outlier-detection methods and six imputation methods, four machine learning models, namely Random Forest, XGBoost, Gaussian Process Regression, and Multi-Layer Perceptron, were trained using 12-, 22-, and 32-year precipitation series. The forecasting task was formulated as one-step-ahead prediction using the three preceding precipitation values and month number as predictors. The final three years of each series were retained as an independent test period, while hyperparameters were optimized exclusively within the training data using TimeSeriesSplit, separately for NSE- and KGE-based optimization. Model performance was evaluated using the Nash–Sutcliffe Efficiency (NSE) and Kling–Gupta Efficiency (KGE), and a factorial ANOVA was used to quantify the relative contribution of the investigated modeling components. The results revealed a strong dependence of modeling-component importance on precipitation regime. Under both NSE and KGE, model selection generally became more influential with increasing mean precipitation, whereas the importance of outlier-removal methods increased toward drier basins. Model selection was dominant in the humid Haraz basin, accounting for 82.0% of the NSE variance and 59.9% of the KGE variance, while outlier-removal methods contributed up to 31.5% under NSE and 54.5% under KGE in the drier basins. In contrast, imputation method generally had a negligible contribution, except in Aras under KGE, where it accounted for 16.2% of the variance. Training data length also showed no consistent direct relationship with improved predictive performance. Overall, the findings demonstrate that no single modeling strategy is optimal across all climatic conditions and highlight the need for basin-specific, climate-aware choices in precipitation prediction workflows.

Bou Azzer-El Graara inlier of Morocco’s Central Anti-Atlas, lying within the Pan-African suture zone, hosts one of the most representative Neoproterozoic ophiolite successions and exploitable deposits of Co, Cu, Ag, and Au minerals. To date, no research has combined quantitative machine learning edge classification with three-dimensional magnetic inversion within a multiscale structural analysis despite decades of work being dedicated to the area. This paper aims to bridge this gap with a four-stage workflow based on high-resolution aeromagnetic data obtained within the Moroccan national program. Horizontal gradient, analytic signal, three orthogonal directional gradients of the first order, tilt derivative, horizontal gradient of tilt angle, and residual RTP map served as the source information for training of a feed-forward ANN through the Levenberg–Marquardt backpropagation algorithm. Two weighting methods were investigated: equal weighting and selective weighting of lithological contact sensitive derivatives. Accuracy was found to be about 86% in both cases, however, very similar AUC values suggest a robust solution rather than any parameterization effect. Weighted probability surfaces reveal both structural corridors well-known from geological maps as well as areas of new structural complexity. At the same time, the volume susceptibility inversion at Foum Zguid Dike zone allows reconstruction of some geometries of this structure down to 2 km depth. The combination of gradient analysis, Euler deconvolution, ANN-based prospectivity analysis, and three-dimensional susceptibility inversion results in a high-resolution structure with predominance of N-S and WNW-ESE faults and outlines new underexplored zones for mineralization of Co–Cu-Ag-Au metals within Bou Azzer region.

Two-phase locking (2PL) is a fundamental and widely used concurrency control protocol. It regulates concurrent access to database data by following a specific sequence of lock acquisition and release during transaction execution, thereby ensuring transaction isolation. However, in strict 2PL, transactions must wait for conflicting transactions to commit and release their locks, which reduces concurrency and system throughput. We have observed that this issue is exacerbated in high-contented workloads at Tencent, where lock contention can severely degrade system performance. While existing optimizations demonstrate some effectiveness in high-contention scenarios, their performance remains insufficient because they suffer from lock contention and hotspot access waits. This paper presents optimizations in lock management implemented in Tencent’s database, TXSQL, with a particular focus on high-contention scenarios. First, we discuss our motivations and the journey toward general lock optimization, which includes lightweight lock management, a copy-free active transaction list, and queue locking mechanisms that effectively enhance concurrency. Second, we introduce a hotspot-aware approach that enables certain highly conflicting transactions to switch to a group locking method, which groups conflicting transactions at a specific hotspot and allows them to execute serially in an uncommitted state within a conflict group without the need for locking, thereby reducing lock contention. Our evaluation shows that under high-contented workloads, TXSQL achieves performance improvements of up to 6.5x and up to 22.3x compared to state-of-the-art methods and systems, respectively.
