期刊：IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering · DOI：10.1109/tkde.2026.3708861 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Structure-sensitive tasks on graphs—such as classification, clustering, and influence analysis—are particularly susceptible to perturbations that distort topological structures and impair functional performance. However, existing robustness evaluations often focus narrowly on single perturbation types or localized noise, limiting their capacity to reflect the compound disruptions observed in real-world networks. To overcome this limitation, we propose HyPer, a hybrid perturbation strategy that combines stochastic and deterministic perturbations within a unified framework. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first attempt to comprehensively simulate complex real-world perturbations. To further mitigate structural degradation, we introduce EnCoDR, a general-purpose robustness enhancement framework designed for structure-sensitive graph tasks. EnCoDR integrates the HyPer strategy into a multi-stage optimization pipeline, which incorporates a multi-scale robustness evaluation mechanism based on the principle of prior irrelevance. This mechanism jointly assesses topological stability and functional resilience, such as the continuity of information propagation. We first assess HyPer’s disruptive capacity through two representative downstream tasks—community detection and influence maximization—demonstrating its effectiveness in simulating complex structural perturbations. Building on this, we extensively instantiate the EnCoDR framework across these tasks, demonstrating its superior performance in preserving structural integrity and ensuring functional continuity under adversarial conditions.

Foundation models have emerged as a dominant paradigm in machine learning, enabling broad generalization and efficient adaptation across diverse tasks and domains. While this paradigm has achieved remarkable success in language and vision data, its extension to structured data remains far less understood. Foundation models for structured data are an emerging yet highly impactful research area with a rapidly growing body of literature. In this survey, we provide a systematic analysis of foundation models for structured data, focusing on tabular, time series, and graph data, covering over 150 representative methods. We analyze the intrinsic properties and inductive biases of structured data, clarify the core concepts of foundation models, and conduct an in-depth analysis of the key challenges that hinder the development of foundation models for structured data. Building on these insights, we organize existing approaches into a coherent taxonomy based on tokenization, architectures, pre-training objectives, and adaptation strategies. Finally, we discuss emerging research directions and open problems, aiming to provide guidance toward more principled and scalable foundation models for structured data.

Compared to the individual classifier, classifier ensemble has great potential to elevate the performance. In this paper, an adaptive progressive optimization ensemble approach (APOEA) is designed for high-dimensional imbalanced data classification. First, a local subview optimization (LSO) is designed, it enables each ensemble member to focus on learning information from different local regions, which helps to achieve dimensionality reduction while maintaining diversity. Granular-Ball Computing (GBC) is an efficient, robust, and highly interpretable multi-granularity representation and computation method. Nonetheless, most feature selection methods based on GBC require considerable time to calculate the significance measures of features or repeatedly generate granular balls, which limits their applicability to high-dimensional data. The graph-based feature selection effectively reduces dimensionality by exploiting feature correlations and redundancies. However, most graph-based feature selection methods are limited to a fine and single granularity knowledge space. Driven by these issues, this paper first proposes the granular-ball divergence-based fuzzy rough set to characterize the uncertain information from a multi-granularity perspective. Finally, a feature selection algorithm is designed to select the best feature subset. The experimental results show that our algorithm achieves better classification performance and higher execution efficiency.

Extensive experiments on two datasets validate the effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed methods.

Additional results on time prediction show that the temporal branch learns meaningful temporal patterns that provide useful signals for the recommendation task.

BPO: Backdoor Purification via Overwriting

期刊：IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering · DOI：10.1109/tkde.2026.3717987 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

To effectively mitigate the threats posed by backdoor attacks, researchers have extensively investigated backdoor purification methods that rely on trigger inversion. Nevertheless, these methods face a fundamental challenge: accurately inverting triggers that faithfully match the true ones is inherently difficult. In particular, such discrepancies between the inverted and true triggers can substantially compromise the effectiveness of the backdoor purification process. To address this limitation, we propose a novel Backdoor Purification scheme via Overwriting (BPO). Specifically, we deliberately inject new backdoors into the suspicious model, thereby overwriting and neutralizing potential pre-existing ones. Since the capacity of a model is inherently limited (that is, the number of backdoors it can accommodate is finite), injecting a sufficiently large number or sufficiently strong backdoors can effectively suppress and replace the pre-existing backdoors. Subsequently, since the defender possesses complete knowledge of the newly injected backdoors, unlearning techniques can be leveraged to remove the associated knowledge and thus obtain a purified model. Moreover, during the unlearning process, parameter sparsification techniques can be employed to further suppress the potential re-emergence of the pre-existing backdoors. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that the proposed scheme consistently reduces the attack success rate of backdoors to below 11%, while limiting the degradation of the original task performance to within 5%, and significantly enhances the purification effectiveness of existing trigger inversion-based methods.

Finally, we take the theoretical analysis and conduct extensive experiments on real-world datasets under both single-task and multi-task data inference situations to validate the effectiveness of the proposed CAA-TDI framework in improving data inference quality.

Inventory datasets comprised 67 implemented stone masonry check-dam locations and 160 historical flood occurrence points, split 70:30 for training and validation. Model performance was evaluated using ROC-AUC, and variable importance was quantified via Jackknife analysis. All three models achieved AUC values in the ‘Very Good’ to ‘Excellent’ range (0.80–0.93) for check dam site suitability, with MaxEnt demonstrating the highest validation AUC (0.93). Jackknife analysis identified Stream Power Index (SPI), Stream Order, Slope, Drainage Density, and Rainfall as the most informative predictors. For flood susceptibility, MaxEnt achieved high predictive accuracy with training and validation AUC values of 0.91 and 0.89, respectively. Stream Order (β = 0.349; p = 0.002) and SPI (β = −1.149; p < 0.001) were the most statistically significant conditioning factors. Spatial modeling identified 147 prioritized sites with substantial potential for watershed dam construction, concentrated along higher-order stream reaches with moderate slope gradients and geotechnically stable lithological substrates. Gypsum-bearing units (Ekgy) and fault-proximate zones were consistently mapped as low suitability across all models. The produced suitability and susceptibility maps provide a science-based spatial framework for prioritizing watershed conservation investments, with wider methodological applicability to comparable mountainous regions globally.
