论文题名

Approximation algorithm for parallel-batch scheduling with resource matching and rejection

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01467-8 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

In this paper, we study a scheduling problem with resource matching and job rejection on m parallel-batch machines. The problem incorporates multiple types of additive resources, where each resource can be allocated to at most one job. Jobs have sizes and release times. Each job is either rejected by paying a certain cost or accepted for processing. Each accepted job consumes exactly one kind of resource, and its actual processing time depends on the allocated resource. The accepted jobs are processed in batches and their total size cannot exceed the machine’s batch capacity. The processing time of a batch is equal to the largest processing time of the jobs in it. The objective is to minimize the sum of the makespan of accepted jobs and the total rejection cost of rejected jobs. We propose three approximation algorithms and establish a worst-case ratio upper bound of 4-2/(∑ _i=1^mb_i), where ∑ _i=1^mb_i denotes the total batch capacity of all machines. Further, we also analyze the worst-case ratios for some special cases.

论文题名

Exact algorithms for solving the constrained convex combinatorial assignment problems with bounds and maximum penalty

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01472-x · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

In this paper, we address the constrained convex combinatorial assignment problem with bounds and maximum penalty (the CCCA-BMP problem, for short). Concretely, given a set M={g1,g2,…,gₘ} of m machines (edge servers) with two bound functions l,u:M→Z0⁺, satisfying the bound requirement l(g_i)≤u(g_i) for each machine g_i∈M, and a set J={a1,a2,…,aₙ} of n independent jobs (computation tasks) with a penalty function p:J→R0⁺, each machine g_i in M receives at least l_i and at most u_i jobs to execute, and each job a_j in J must be either executed on some machine g_i∈M with its processing time c_ij>0, or rejected (executed by the cloud computing center) with its penalty p_j that we must pay for. We consider the CCCA-BMP problem and its variation, i.e., (1) The CCCA-BMP problem is to find an assignment scheme of these n jobs that satisfies the constraints as mentioned-above, the objective is to minimize the convex combination of the total processing time of the executed jobs and the maximum penalty of rejected jobs; (2) The penalized convex combinatorial assignment problem with bounds (the PCCA-B problem, for short) is to find an assignment scheme of these n jobs that satisfies the constraints as mentioned-above, the objective is to minimize the convex combination of the total processing time of executed jobs and maximum penalty of executed jobs. As our main contributions, we present two exact combinatorial algorithms to solve the CCCA-BMP problem and the PCCA-B problem, respectively. In addition, we give numerical examples to illustrate the execution processes of the two algorithms we proposed.

In this paper, we address the constrained strong Steiner connectivity augmentation (CSStCA) problem. Concretely, given a weighted digraph D=(V,A;w), where w:A→R⁺ is a weight function, K (⊆V) is a set of k fixed vertices (called terminals), and Tₖ=(Vₖ,Aₖ) is a strongly connected Steiner subgraph (of D), i.e., Tₖ contains a directed path from s to t, where s and t are any pair of two distinct terminals in K (⊆Vₖ), we are asked to find an arc-set A^′⊆A∖Aₖ to satisfy the constraints that, given each arc e∈Aₖ, the digraph D[A^′∪Aₖ∖{e}] reduced by A^′∪Aₖ∖{e} is still a strongly connected Steiner subgraph (of D), the objective is to minimize the summation of weights of all arcs in A^′, where the summation is taken among all arc-sets to satisfy the constraints as mentioned-above. Especially, given a set K={x,y} (⊆V), we refer to he CSStCA roblem as to the constrained directed circuit augmentation (CDCA) problem, and given a set K=V, we refer to the CSStCA problem as to the constrained strong connectivity augmentation (CSCA) problem. We obtain the following three important results. (1) We design a 2-approximation algorithm to solve the CDCA problem in time O(mn); (2) Using the preceding algorithm in (1) as a subroutine for many times, we present a 2k-approximation algorithm to solve the CSStCA problem in time O(kmn); (3) We provide a 2-approximation algorithm to solve the CSCA problem in time O(n²(m+nlog⁡n)log⁡n).

Minimum power partial cover meets fairness: improved approximation guarantees

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01477-6 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

In this paper, we study the minimum power partial cover problem with fairness constraints (MinPPCF). Let U be a set of m points and S be a set of n base stations in the plane. Each station s∈S can adjust its transmission power level p(s), which determines a circular coverage region of radius r(s) satisfying p(s)=c⋅r(s)^α. We say that a point u∈U is covered by a base station s if the distance between u and s is at most r(s), i.e., d(u,s)≤r(s). The set U is partitioned into l groups, U_i,∀i∈[l]. Given parameters k≤m and k_i≤|U_i|, the goal of the MinPPCF problem is to assign power level to the stations such that at least k points in total, and at least k_i points from each group U_i, are covered, while minimizing the total power consumption. Although this problem has been previously studied, we improve the approximation ratio to O(αln⁡l). Our approach is a primal-dual algorithm which is based on a strengthened LP relaxation, combined with a relaxed independent set technique. Furthermore, we show that the price of fairness could be infinite.

论文题名

Compact SAT encoding for power peak minimization in assembly line balancing

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01471-y · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

The Simple Assembly Line Balancing Problem with Power Peak Minimization (SALB3PM) minimizes maximum instantaneous power usage while assigning n tasks to m workstations and determining execution schedules within given cycle time constraints. This NP-hard problem couples workstation assignment, temporal sequencing, and power aggregation, presenting significant computational challenges for exact optimization methods. Existing Boolean Satisfiability (SAT) and Maximum Satisfiability (MaxSAT) approaches rely on pairwise station and time encodings that can generate quadratic numbers of clauses in the workstation or cycle-time dimensions. We introduce a Compact SAT Encoding (CSE) using sequential counter techniques to reduce assignment, scheduling, and precedence encodings from pairwise to linear-size formulations in these dimensions. We instantiate four optimization variants over the resulting CSE model: clause-blocking SAT refinement, pseudo-Boolean bound tightening, MaxSAT, and ordered peak-indicator incremental SAT. Comprehensive experimental evaluation on benchmark instances demonstrates consistent performance improvements over state-of-the-art approaches, including higher coverage and lower cumulative solving time under the standard timeout. The encoding principles generalize to other assembly line balancing variants and broader scheduling problems with precedence constraints.

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01449-w · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

This study examines demand prediction and information sharing in medical industry, emphasizing R&D and logistics. It shows that effective information sharing, driven by R&D and logistical efficiency, boosts supply chain performance. Utilizing Stackelberg game and reverse deduction, this paper establishes a two-part compensation contract coordination mode under decentralized decision-making, considering the impact of R&D and logistics distribution as endogenous variables, and benchmarking against cooperative decision-making. Then, through numerical simulation and sensitivity changes under different decision models, findings suggest that dealer might opt against investing if the big data technology level is inadequate. Additionally, the design contract plays an important role in promoting the R&D effort, logistics level, and profits of various members.

论文题名

Submodular maximization under knapsack and partition matroid constraints

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01470-z · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Consider the problem of maximizing a monotone submodular function subject to knapsack constraint and partition matroid constraint. Specifically, we consider a ground set V that is divided into k disjoint classes. To quantify the imbalance of cost value within each class, we introduce a key parameter, the maximum imbalance parameter λ, defined as the maximum ratio of cost values between any two elements within the same class. We design a modified greedy algorithm that returns the better solution between a greedy candidate and the best single element. We prove that this algorithm achieves an approximation ratio of min{1/(1+λ)(1−e^(−(1+λ)/2)),1/(2+λ)}. Furthermore, we demonstrate the performance limits of our algorithm by constructing two types of worst-case instances, establishing upper bound of 1/(λ−1) for large λ and numerical upper bound for small λ. These results establish theoretical boundaries in terms of the parameter λ, quantifying the gap relative to our derived approximation guarantee.

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01474-9 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

A graph G=(V,E) is k-connected if |V|>k and G∖X remains connected for any vertex subset X with |X|<k. A subset X⊆V is a (k, m)-connected dominating set if it is an m-fold dominating set and induces a k-connected subgraph. In this paper, we investigate a new variant of this problem, referred to as the minimum (1, m)-connected dominating set with labeling problem. Given a connected graph G=(V,E) with a vertex-labeling function l:V→L, the goal is to find a (1, m)-connected dominating set with the minimum number of distinct labels. We propose a greedy approximation algorithm and prove that its approximation ratio is at most ln⁡(m|V|)+span(G)+1, where span(G) denotes the maximum number of connected components among all subgraphs induced by a single label.

An approximation algorithm for solving the many-visits prize-collecting path traveling salesman problem

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01484-7 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

In this paper, we consider the many-visits prize-collecting path traveling salesman problem. The input consists of a complete undirected graph G=(V,E;w,r,p;s,t) with self-loops, two distinct fixed vertices s,t∈V, an edge weight function w:E→R_≥0 satisfying the triangle inequality, a visiting requirement function r:V→Z_>0, and a penalty function p:V→R_≥0. We are asked to find an s-t walk W in G such that the two terminals s and t are visited at least rₛ and rₜ times, respectively, and, for every vertex v∈V∖{s,t}, either W visits v at least r_v times and no penalty is paid for v, or W visits v fewer than r_v times and the penalty p_v is paid. The objective is to minimize the total weight of W plus the total penalties paid. We design a polynomial-time α-approximation algorithm for solving this problem, where α=1/(1−e^(−3/5))<2.217.

This paper studies the min-max correlation clustering problem with penalties, a variant of the correlation clustering problem. Two versions of the problem are considered: one on complete graphs and the other on complete bipartite graphs. In both versions, each vertex in the graph can be clustered or penalized, with the goal of minimizing the sum of the total disagreement edges and penalty cost at the worst vertex. In the complete graph version, the range of minimization goal is all the vertices, whereas in the complete bipartite graph version, the range of minimization goal is one of the two vertex sets. We provide integer and linear programming relaxations for these two versions. Furthermore, we present 19-approximation algorithms for this problem under both versions.

The characterizations and complexity of Roman {2}-domination number and 2-domination number in graphs

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01441-4 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Given a graph G=(V,E) and a function b:V→{0,1,2}. If b satisfies ∑_z∈N_G(w)b(z)≥2 for every vertex w with b(w)=0, where N_G(w) is the neighborhood of w in G, then b is called a Roman {2}-dominating function of G. The Roman {2}-domination number γ_{R2}(G) of G is the minimum value ∑_w∈Vb(w) among all Roman {2}-dominating functions b of G. A set S⊆V is a 2-dominating set of G if |N_G(w)∩S|≥2 for each w∈V∖S. The minimum cardinality among all 2-dominating sets of G is called the 2-domination number γ2(G) of G. For any graph G, Chellali et al. (2016) showed that γ_{R2}(G)≤γ2(G). In this article, firstly, we provide a characterization for the trees T with γ_{R2}(T)=γ2(T). Secondly, for a tree T, we provide a lower bound of γ_{R2}(T) relying on γ2(T) and the number of leaves l(T) in T: γ_{R2}(T)≥γ2(T)−l(T)+2, and a characterization for the trees T with γ_{R2}(T)=γ2(T)−l(T)+2. Finally, we prove that it is NP-hard to determine whether γ_{R2}(G) and γ2(G) are equal for a given bipartite graph G.

论文题名

Approximation algorithms for k-submodular maximization under fairness constraints and size constraints

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01440-5 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Submodular optimization is an important part of combinatorial optimization, and meanwhile, it has been widely used in the fields of economy, computer science, etc. In recent years, many generalizations of the submodular maximization problem have been proposed, such as the k-submodular maximization problem, which considers not only whether the elements of the ground set are selected, but also which set of k-set they are selected into. Many machine learning problems, including influence maximization with k kinds of topics and sensor placement with k kinds of sensors, can be modeled as k-submodular maximization models. In practical applications, elements usually have certain attributes, such as gender, age, race, and so on. Based on these attributes we can group elements in the ground set. For fairness, we want the number of elements in each group to be relatively balanced, which introduces group fairness. Group fairness can be conceptualized in various ways, but typically falls into two primary categories: equity-fairness and equality-fairness. At present, the existing algorithms on k-submodular do not consider group fairness constraints, which can result in the number of elements in some groups being too high or too low. This motivates us to study k-submodular maximization under group fairness constraints. In this paper, we consider intersection constraints of two different categories of group fairness constraints and total size constraint, respectively. For the k-submodular maximization problem under equity-fairness constraints and total size constraint, we design an offline approximation algorithm with an approximate ratio of 1/2-ε for the case of monotone, and an approximate ratio of 1/3-ε for non-monotone. For the k-submodular maximization problem under equality-fairness constraints and total size constraint, we get an approximate ratio of 1/2 for the case of monotone and an approximate ratio of 0.041 for the case of non-monotone.

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01434-3 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Given an edge-weighted undirected connected graph G=(V,E,ρ,X,Y), where ρ:E→R⁺∪{0} is an edge-weight function, X⊂V is a subset of clients, and Y⊂V is a subset of candidates, and a positive integer k<|Y|, the k-Supplier Problem (k SP) asks for an optimal subset of Y of cardinality at most k to minimize the radius from X to the subset. In this paper, we focus on the case of X∩Y≠∅,X,Y, and consider the scenario where the shortest path distances d(⋅,⋅) in G satisfy a parameterized triangle inequality between X and Y, i.e., d(x,y)+d(y,z)≥α⋅d(x,z),∀x,y,z∈{u,v,w},x≠y,y≠z,z≠x, where 1≤α≤2 is a parameter, for any three distinct vertices, v,u∈X and w∈Y. We present a two-stage dual approximation algorithm ALG for the kSP with parameter triangle inequality between X and Y. If it stops at the end of Stage 1 then it achieves a 2/α-approximation, and if it stops at the end of Stage 2 then it achieves a (2/(α²)+1/α)-approximation. ALG runs in a polynomial time and the above two parameterized performance factors of it are both strictly monotonic decreasing with respect to the value of parameter α. For the kSP instances with parameterized triangle inequality having 1<α≤2, it is implied by 1≤2/α<2 and 1≤2/(α²)+1/α<3 that ALG has better approximation ratios than the previously best polynomial-time 3-approximation algorithm of Hochbaum and Shmoys (J. ACM. 33: 533–550, 1986). Furthermore, regardless of the parameterized aspect, ALG achieves a 2-approximation if it stops at the end of Stage 1 and a 3-approximation if it stops at the end of Stage 2, for the general kSP.

期刊：Journal of Combinatorial Optimization · DOI：10.1007/s10878-026-01427-2 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

The Maximally Diverse Grouping Problem (MDGP) is the problem of assigning a set of elements to mutually disjoint groups in order to maximise the overall diversity between the elements. The MDGP is often formulated as an Integer Quadratic Programme (IQP). Because the MDGP is NP-complete, most studies have focused on heuristic solution approaches, as compared to exact solution approaches, to the problem. Although heuristic solution approaches are often used in practice, these approaches do not guarantee a global optimal solution. Conversely, although exact solution approaches are not often used in practice, these approaches guarantee a global optimal solution, and therefore serve as useful benchmarks for the performance of heuristics. As such, a few studies have formulated the MDGP as an Integer Linear Programme (ILP), which can be solved using exact solution approaches. The present paper proposes two new ILP formulations, and compares their performances against existing formulations. The proposed formulations can be used to establish useful benchmarks for the performance of heuristics in a broader range of applications moving forward.

This paper studies a bi-criteria parallel machine scheduling problem with uncertain processing times to minimize makespan and total completion time. We adopt a scenario-based approach for uncertainty and assume that a scenario index can take any real value in an interval. This index value can represent, for example, the amount of congestion in a network. We assume that the job processing times are non-decreasing polynomial functions of fixed degrees with respect to the scenario index. To tackle multi-criteria optimization in the presence of uncertainty, researchers have proposed the concept of multi-scenario efficient set (Botte and Schöbel 2019, Engau and Sigler 2020). Based on a dynamic programming framework, with carefully designed states, we obtain a Fully Polynomial-Time Approximation Scheme (FPTAS) for the problem.
