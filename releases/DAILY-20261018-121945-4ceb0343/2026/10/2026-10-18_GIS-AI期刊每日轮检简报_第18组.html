The construction of expressive space: exploring the relationship between social media expression and the spatial practices of heritage towns

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2026.2738726 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

The rise of social media has reshaped how people perceive and engage with cultural heritage spaces. This study examines the impact of social media expressions on the spatial practices and evolution of heritage towns in China. Taking three historic towns as cases, the research integrates Weibo User-generated Content (UGC) with Point of Interest (POI) data to examine the spatial correspondence, thematic structures, and predictive potential of digital expressions. Combining spatial matching, BERTopic-based image topic modeling, and logistic regression, the study identifies three findings. First, social media expressions form a platform-mediated expressive space that diverges from official POI distributions, revealing an uneven reconfiguration of spatial visibility and heritage boundaries. Second, while social expressions across historic towns exhibit synchronized rhythms shaped by national festivals, they also display significant local differentiation reflecting cultural and governance models. Third, the concentration of thematic expressions and the influence of opinion leaders significantly increase the potential of spatial renewal, implying that digital expression may serve as an indicative signal of potential physical transformation. These findings highlight a shift from institutionally defined representations of heritage space toward platform-mediated expressive spaces, suggesting that social expressions can serve as dynamic indicators for micro-renewal and cultural planning in historic towns.

论文题名

A new framework for global 3D data organization: sphere octal hexahedron mesh

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2026.2736830 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Research on three-dimensional (3D) discrete global grid systems (DGGSs) is important for applications requiring simulation or analysis on a sphere. Existing 3D DGGSs face challenges in data integration, uneven shape and size of cells, and complex subdivision rules. In this paper, we propose a new 3D global grid called sphere octal hexahedron mesh (SOHM) by extending the quaternary quadrangle mesh along the radial direction and designing encoding and decoding algorithms for SOHM. SOHM has simple subdivision rules, cells of analogous shapes and sizes, and simple relationships between a grid address and its geographic coordinates. SOHM is suitable to serve as a fundamental framework for organizing global 3D data across all latitudes, from deep subsurface to high altitudes. We apply SOHM to satellite orbit tracking and temperature field visualization. Experimental results demonstrate that SOHM achieves smaller convergence values, more analogous shapes, and better encoding and decoding efficiency than the sphere degenerated octree grid, which facilitates efficient management and manipulation of spatial data.

Because many existing tools are not built around classroom workflows, they may fail to sustain inquiry or support effective instruction. This article shows how a user-centered design (UCD) approach can improve educational mapping through the collaborative development of Roots & Migrants, a web-based map platform for teaching and learning about U.S. historical population dynamics. We argue that educational interactive maps should be designed as instructional systems grounded in classroom practice, pedagogical goals, and data limitations, with implications beyond history education.

论文题名

Evaluating intent-driven color generation: a framework for assessing LLM-assisted cartographic design

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2026.2727154 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Large language models (LLMs) offer new possibilities for adaptive cartographic design, yet how they interpret user intent remains unclear. This study evaluates how stated analytical goals influence LLM-generated color schemes for thematic maps, assessing three perceptual dimensions: whether colors form a clear sequence matching the data hierarchy (order), whether categories are visually distinct from one another (differentiation), and whether related values share similar or thematically cohesive colors (association). Using a synthetic dataset of 20 lakes classified by pollution level (Low, Medium, High), we tested three LLMs across 12 prompt conditions varying in task framing and analytical emphasis. GPT-5 demonstrated systematic intent sensitivity: order prompts produced cohesive color schemes, differentiation prompts generated multi-hue palettes with greater separation, and lightness encoding reversed depending on whether users emphasized high or low pollution. DeepSeekV3.2 produced perceptually valid schemes but showed no adaptation to user intent, generating fixed red-yellow-green palettes regardless of prompting. Llama3.2 did not produce complete outputs. These findings suggest intent-sensitive color generation requires reasoning capabilities not uniformly present across models. We propose an evaluation framework linking user intent to measurable color properties and discuss implications for exploratory mapping systems where analytical goals shift dynamically.

XFlowMap: cross-scale generalization and mapping of massive origin-destination data

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2026.2738731 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Mapping large origin-destination (OD) datasets remains challenging because flow maps quickly become cluttered, salient flow patterns often emerge at different spatial scales, and existing methods frequently depend on predefined aggregation units or manual generalization. This paper presents XFlowMap, a methodology for the cross-scale generalization and mapping of massive OD data. Specifically, it integrates cross-scale flow pattern (cluster) detection, automated flow map generalization, and a new cartographic representation for analyzing and visualizing complex origin-destination flow structures. The approach detects salient flow patterns at their appropriate origin and destination scales respectively, extracts high-level structures, and generates a new flow map representation that supports holistic interpretation of complex origin-destination flow patterns. A scan-statistic-based procedure is developed to evaluate and generalize cross-scale flow clusters. The detected clusters are then visualized using a flow symbol that integrates location, direction, strength, and OD scales in a single representation. The method is robust to sparse and noisy datasets, and enables comparative mapping of stratified flow data. Experiments with synthetic data, U.S. migration data, and taxi OD trips data demonstrate that the method effectively extracts meaningful cross-scale flow patterns and produces clear, information-rich flow maps for large mobility datasets, supporting both static presentation and interactive exploration.

Development and user experience evaluation of an interactive application for temporal analysis of historical maps

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2026.2727157 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Despite the growing availability of digitized historical maps, users still face a key bottleneck: interfaces rarely support efficient comparison across time periods and map types. This paper presents the development and evaluation of web-based application designed for exploring and comparing historical maps and aerial imagery. The platform integrates 72 sources and implements four comparison modes: single, swipe, multiview, and overlay. User experience was evaluated in a laboratory study with 54 participants using User Experience Questionnaire, eye-tracking, and interaction logging. Results indicate a highly positive overall user experience, with strong ratings for perspicuity, efficiency, and attractiveness. Eye-tracking and log data further showed that participants primarily focused on map content, consulted interface elements only briefly, and used comparison modes adaptively depending on task demands. The evaluation also identified several interaction issues relevant to interface refinement, particularly concerning control discoverability, spatial orientation, and feedback during missing-data or loading states. The evaluation is framed as a diagnostic user experience assessment with behavioral observation, used to identify usability-relevant issues and support design improvement. A case study demonstrates the application’s potential for analyzing long-term urban transformation. More broadly, the study shows how empirical UX evaluation can inform the refinement of publicly accessible historical map portals.

Adaptive graph construction and graph contrastive learning for geospatial feature embedding

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2026.2738733 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Geospatial features serve as a cornerstone in urban planning and location-based services. Effective geospatial feature embedding is crucial for downstream geospatial Deep Neural Networks (DNN) applications. Currently, geospatial feature representation learning approaches rely on image or textual data to implicitly encode specific geospatial features, such as Points of Interest (POIs), roads, and urban areas, and struggle to effectively model spatial proximity relationships between imbalanced-distribution geospatial features. Moreover, the difficulty in effectively selecting appropriate geographic feature negative samples during model training poses a challenge to traditional representation learning methods. To address these limitations, we propose a novel Geospatial Adaptive Contrastive Embedding approach to extract neighboring features in an imbalanced-distribution space using Delaunay triangulation. It can effectively capture the natural proximity relationship through analysis the distribution characteristics of the geospatial features without setting a threshold. Graph contrastive learning is employed to encode geospatial features into low-dimensional feature vectors through self-supervised spatial similarity preservation. Finally, a comprehensive evaluation of the generated spatial proximity graphs and the learned representation are performed by using through three real-world geospatial datasets. The results demonstrate that our approach successfully learns an invariant and discriminative representation of geospatial features by constructing an effective spatial proximity graph.

论文题名

面向虚拟现实内容的用户体验质量评价综述

期刊：中国图象图形学报 · DOI：10.11834/jig.260101 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

随着多媒体技术的飞速发展，虚拟现实（virtual reality，VR）技术以及沉浸式媒体日益受到重视，已逐渐成为一个重要的研究和开发领域。由360°相机捕获的VR内容，如全景图像和全景视频，相比传统2D视频图像能够提供更为丰富和完整的周围环境细节。凭借其全方位视角与沉浸式的观看体验，全景图像和全景视频能够增强用户的真实性、互动性和沉浸感，因此已在众多领域中得到广泛应用。然而，VR内容的固有球形特性对其存储与传输提出了巨大挑战。此外，在内容采集到终端显示的复杂处理流程中，VR内容都可能引入导致质量下降的潜在因素，这些因素共同影响用户的最终体验。因此，准确描述和量化整个处理流程中感知损伤，并提出有效评价用户体验质量的方法至关重要。早期用户体验质量评价主要依赖吞吐量、带宽、延迟、抖动和丢包率等网络性能参数，但难以全面反映多媒体服务中网络波动对用户体验的影响。为弥补这一不足，体验质量作为关键方案被提出，通常指用户使用应用或服务时的满意程度，受用户期望、个性特征以及当前状态影响。体验质量强调以用户为中心，能更准确地反映整体体验。其量化过程将系统性能与人类感知转化为可测指标，并可通过主观和客观两种方式进行测量。尽管已有不少研究致力于面向VR内容的用户体验质量评价，但目前仍缺乏对这一领域现有方法的系统综述，这一定程度上制约了对该领域的深入理解与分析。本文对面向虚拟现实内容的用户体验质量的最新研究进行全面梳理，旨在推动对该领域的统一认识。首先，通过识别并归类当前虚拟现实内容体验质量评价方法的关键特征，建立了一套分类体系与特征描述，从而构建出用于组织现有方法的结构化框架。进一步地，本文将虚拟现实内容体验质量评价技术划分为两个主要阶段，即主观评价与客观评价。一方面。本文对各类VR内容主观数据集的特点及其构建流程进行了分析，并探讨了影响因素、测试方法、环境设置及评分步骤等关键要素。另一方面，本文根据VR内容的不同表达形式，将面向VR内容的客观体验质量评价方法划分为基于二维平面、基于球面和基于视口的方法三类。其中，基于2D平面的方法主要补偿投影伪影，分为启发式方法和数据驱动方法。基于球面的方法直接在球域操作，采用全局或局部采样策略。基于视口的方法从多视角评价内容质量，按采样策略分为基于规则、关键点和扫视路径三类。在完成各类模型的分类阐述后，还深入剖析了不同方法的优缺点。此外，本文对面向VR内容体验质量评价模型性能的评价指标进行了归纳，并探讨了客观评价模型的实际应用场景。最后，本文对当前面向VR内容的用户体验质量主客观评价研究进行总结，剖析该领域尚未解决的研究局限以及当前面临的障碍，并指出未来可能的发展方向。

期刊：中国图象图形学报 · DOI：10.11834/jig.260516 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

水下图像增强（underwater image enhancement，UIE）旨在从受吸收、散射、悬浮颗粒及非均匀照明影响的退化图像中恢复清晰、自然的场景图像，以满足下游视觉任务需求。近年来，生成式网络已成为 UIE 研究的重要范式，但现有方法在生成主干、监督方式、物理约束、推理效率及评测口径上差异显著，造成各方法间缺乏可比性。本文围绕基于生成式网络的 UIE 方法开展系统性综述：从水下成像退化机理出发，分析传统物理先验、数据驱动学习与生成式建模之间的演进关系；将现有方法归纳为 生成对抗网络（generative adversarial network，GAN）、Transformer 与 Diffusion 三类范式，并从监督方式、生成机制、物理/语义约束及部署成本等维度进行跨方法对比；结合 水下图像增强基准数据集（underwater image enhancement benchmark dataset，UIEB）、U45与TEST-U90等数据集的定量结果，探讨 峰值信噪比（peak signal-to-noise ratio，PSNR）、结构相似性指数（structural similarity index measure，SSIM）、水下彩色图像质量评价（underwater color image quality evaluation，UCIQE）、水下图像质量评价（underwater image quality measure，UIQM） 等指标之间的分离现象及其对方法选择的影响。实验结果表明，GAN、Transformer 与 Diffusion 分别适用于实时增强、全局一致性建模与高保真恢复等不同需求。未来 UIE 研究应进一步关注复杂退化耦合、跨域泛化、评价协议标准化、轻量化部署及可信可控生成等关键问题。本文可为生成式 UIE 方法的分类理解、实验设计与工程部署提供理论依据与实践指导。

期刊：中国图象图形学报 · DOI：10.11834/jig.260161 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

目的针对实时检测Transformer（real-time detection transformer，RT-DETR）在极低比特后训练量化下因权重与激活联合离散化而导致检测精度显著退化的问题，研究一种无需重新训练、适用于边缘部署的低比特量化方法。方法提出权重激活协同自适应量化（weight-activation co-adaptive quantization，WCAQ）框架。该方法构建端到端、无需重新训练的后训练量化流程：权重侧采用分组伪量化以抑制静态离散误差，激活侧将量化置于算子输出端并引入样本自适应尺度调节，结合敏感分支保护和轻量级逐层重构，对双侧量化引起的联合误差进行补偿。结果在COCO 2017（Common Objects in Context）上，WCAQ在8位和6位量化下平均精度（average precision，AP）分别达到46.2%和44.8%，与全精度基线高度相当；在4位极低比特下仍保持28.0%的AP，较最优方法QRT-DETR提高5.4%，克服了传统分离量化导致的精度坍塌。整个量化流程仅需19分钟，较QRT-DETR的110分钟降低约82.7%。结论WCAQ在无需重新训练的前提下实现了接近无损的8位量化和稳定的6位量化，并显著改善了4位量化下RT-DETR的部署可用性，为检测Transformer的边缘低比特部署提供了高效可靠的后训练量化方案。本文方法已发布于ScienceDB，访问链接为：https：//doi.org/10.57760/sciencedb.j00240.00092。

Source identification in propagation graphs is an inverse problem that aims to recover the initial infected or activated nodes from an observed propagation snapshot. This paper studies a static, structure-driven setting in which temporal traces, observer timestamps, and large labeled diffusion histories are unavailable. Voting and ranking-fusion methods are often motivated by the ensemble-learning intuition that several imperfect signals can be more useful after aggregation than any single signal alone. The study tests whether this intuition also helps multi-source localization on propagation graphs, where the individual signals are structural node heuristics rather than learned classifiers. The evaluation covers ten structural heuristics, four ensemble aggregation families, and four implemented source-localization baselines across 22 network-by-propagation cases and 198 saved propagation instances. The analysis separates fixed-method comparisons from diagnostic headroom, compares structural ensembles with non-structural source-localization baselines, and examines whether source counts can be estimated from score gaps or score thresholds rather than supplied as an oracle k. The results support a qualified conclusion. Structural ensembles expose a statistically reliable direction of improvement over selected single-heuristic references, but practical fixed-method gains are modest and depend on how member weights or configurations are chosen. Average-distance and topological centrality are the strongest recurring structural signals, suggesting that future methods should model proximity and graph-position evidence more explicitly rather than enumerate more voting combinations. Adaptive epsilon-gap and threshold rules partially control source-count error, but they do not remove the source-count problem under static snapshots. Overall, ensemble ranking fusion is useful as a calibrated diagnostic and sometimes as a practical aggregation layer, while richer temporal information, validation data, or more principled source-count estimators are still needed for robust deployment.

Although prior research has documented these associations in clinical settings, the underlying psychosocial and behavioral dynamics of such co-occurrence remain underexplored in non-clinical contexts, such as social media health narratives. These findings illustrate how social media discourse can complement clinical research in characterizing high-risk populations, with potential implications for the design of targeted digital mental health interventions for adolescents and young adults at the intersection of eating disorders and suicide risk.

Deep learning for evolving threat detection in online social networks: a PRISMA-based systematic review

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01649-6 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Social media platforms have become prime targets for disinformation, fake accounts, and cyberbullying. Deep learning approaches (CNN, RNN, LSTM, BERT) have demonstrated high performance in detecting these OSN threats, achieving over 90% accuracy on benchmark datasets. However, this performance is based on static, outdated data (2015–2018), while malicious actors may continuously modify their behaviors, tactics, and content in response to detection mechanisms. However, the extent to which these models remain robust under temporally evolving threat patterns is less systematically investigated. This systematic review follows the PRISMA 2020 guidelines to analyze how deep learning approaches handle (or ignore) evolving OSN threats on social media. A search of five databases (IEEE, ACM, Scopus, Springer, Google Scholar) identified 450 records. After removing duplicates and screening titles, abstracts, and full texts, 35 studies published between 2020 and 2026 were selected for in-depth qualitative analysis. The results reveal three major findings. First, hybrid and transformer-based architectures are frequently represented among the reviewed approaches, with some studies reporting accuracy values above 90% on their respective benchmark datasets. Second, the distribution of the reviewed studies is concentrated on a limited number of OSN threat types. Fake news represents 31.43% of the corpus (11/35 studies), followed by cyberbullying (25.71%, 9/35) and social bots (14.29%, 5/35). Fake accounts account for 5.71% (2/35), while the remaining threat categories each represent 2.86% of the corpus. This distribution indicates that research attention is unevenly distributed across OSN threat types. Third, and most critically, explicit temporal validation and adaptation mechanisms remain limited and heterogeneous across the reviewed studies. None of the 35 studies explicitly reported a systematic chronological train/test validation protocol. Some studies nevertheless used newer datasets or considered evolutionary characteristics, which we coded separately from temporal validation. Overall, the extent to which researchers account for threat evolution varies considerably, ranging from indirect or partial consideration to more explicit evolutionary modelling. Similarly, mechanisms that enable models to adapt to changes over time remain limited and are not consistently reported. These findings highlight a methodological gap between conventional benchmark-based evaluation and the need to assess model robustness in OSN environments that continuously evolve. We refer to this gap as the Evolution Optimization Paradox (EOP), which is used in this review as a synthesis framework rather than as a machine-learning theory. To address this gap, we propose a research agenda structured around five directions: (1) collecting recent datasets (2024–2026), (2) systematically adopting temporal validation, (3) developing adaptive models (including concept drift detection and online learning), (4) evaluating cross-platform generalization, and (5) standardizing metrics for reproducibility. This review contributes to the literature by offering a novel critical analysis of existing approaches, identifying a fundamental gap that has been overlooked, and providing a roadmap for developing robust detection systems to address the rapid evolution of online OSN threats.

Political discourse on social networks continuously evolves across electoral cycles and communicative contexts, producing measurable changes in discourse distributions over time. This work introduces an unsupervised framework for detecting and quantifying these changes through multidimensional drift analysis. The approach models discourse evolution across semantic, lexical, thematic, and syntactic dimensions by combining complementary drift detection techniques over text, enabling systematic and reproducible analyses of discourse change. The framework is applied to multilingual political discourse published on party manifestos and Facebook between 2014 and 2023 across multiple electoral cycles and institutional levels in Spain and France. Results show that the proposed framework is able to detect, in a fully unsupervised manner, structural patterns consistent with known dynamics of political communication, including stable distinctions between campaign and routine discourse. At the same time, the framework provides measurable and objective indicators that enable more systematic analyses of discourse evolution across comparable political scenarios.
