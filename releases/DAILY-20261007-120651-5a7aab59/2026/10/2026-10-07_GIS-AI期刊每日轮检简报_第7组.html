综合是地理学存在的根本。综合地理学建立在地理学三大分支学科综合研究之上，作为地理学的新兴二级学科，旨在回答地理学传统分支学科难以独立解决的宏观、复杂的综合性命题，是聚焦人地耦合系统整体结构、功能及其演化规律，探究其变化驱动机制与优化调控的科学。综合地理学聚焦自然环境与人类社会的相互作用，将人地耦合系统作为核心研究对象，主要研究内容包括地理要素综合、地理过程综合和地理区域综合。地理要素综合不仅关注自然地理要素综合和人文地理要素综合，更强调自然与人文要素的综合。地理过程综合关注人类圈与其他圈层的相互作用，强调自然地理过程和人文地理过程的综合，发展地理格局与地理过程的耦合方法。地理区域综合注重地理区域的综合解析、综合划分和系统辨识。地理大数据、地理人工智能和人地耦合系统模型是综合地理学研究的重要研究方法。科学提出协调平衡经济、社会、环境的调控路径支撑可持续发展，是综合地理学在国家治理体系和全球议题中的重要应用场景。总之，综合地理学能够推动地理学综合思想转化为具体的知识结构，其理论与方法在解决人类面临的资源环境和社会经济可持续发展等问题中发挥核心支撑作用，是地理学综合思想不断走向成熟的标志。

论文题名

Transcending natural evolution in the age of artificial intelligence

期刊：Science China Information Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11432-026-5041-7 · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

Artificial intelligence (AI) is undergoing a fundamental transition from digital representational models to embodied, physically situated systems. This position paper characterises this shift as the crossing of an embodiment threshold, where Al’s operational capacity becomes integrated with physical environments and neural substrates. We argue that this transition is defined by a profound evolutionary tempo mismatch between rapid algorithmic iteration and the millennial-scale inertia of biological evolution. By synthesising advances in flexible bioelectronics and high-bandwidth neural decoding, we examine the biophysical mechanisms that could enable human-machine symbiosis while exposing the thermodynamic and material constraints that accompany this trajectory. In particular, we contrast the brain’s highly efficient metabolic architecture with the resource-intensive scaling of silicon-based computation, highlighting the risk that the infrastructure required for large-scale augmentation may produce durable socio-technical stratification. 论文题名

Practical phase-coding side-channel-secure quantum key distribution

期刊：Science China Information Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11432-025-4932-x · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Quantum key distribution (QKD) provides theoretically secure solutions to realize remote key distribution. However, there are many kinds of device loopholes giving rise to a great obstacle to its practical security. Some existing studies have addressed certain vulnerabilities. But it is hard to eliminate all potential loopholes while maintaining high performance. In this article, we propose a new QKD protocol called the phase-coding side-channel-secure (PC-SCS) protocol inspired by the sending-or-not-sending SCS protocol. This protocol can be immune to all uncorrelated side channels of the source part and all loopholes of the measurement part. A finite-key security analysis against coherent attacks of the new protocol is given. The proposed protocol only requires the modulation of two phases, which can avoid the challenge of preparing perfect vacuum states. Numerical simulation shows that a practical transmission distance of 300 km in fiber can be realized by the PC-SCS protocol.

A heterogeneous CIM/CNM architecture for LMs utilizing speed/density-optimized 3D CAA-IGZO DRAMs

期刊：Science China Information Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11432-025-4938-9 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Large artificial intelligence models (LMs) based on the Transformer have demonstrated superior performance in many applications. The encoder (prefill) and decoder parts of LMs show significant differences in the compute-to-memory ratio and the memory speed/density requirement. Recently, the emerging Indium-Gallium-Zinc-Oxide (IGZO) transistor has become a promising memory candidate for LM architectures, featuring ultra-low leakage current and high-density monolithic 3D fabrication. Unfortunately, prior studies primarily emphasize device-level fabrication, while overlooking critical circuit-level requirements for practical applications and the parasitic effects inherent in practical manufacturing. This paper proposes 3DIG-CXM, the first 3D-IGZO-based computing-in/near-memory (CIM/CNM) architecture for LMs. The highlights include (1) for compute-bound LM encoder, we propose a speed-optimized 3D-3T0C IGZO DRAM array that supports 2D full-layer-copy fast read ability to satisfy the high-performance CIM engines, enabling 3.45× higher read frequency; (2) for memory-bound LM decoder, we propose a density-optimized 3D-2T0C IGZO DRAM array featuring underneath peripheral and CNM circuits for ultra-high storage density; (3) a 3D parasitic effect evaluation method is built to explore the maximum 3D dimensions; (4) a pipelined workflow based on the speed/density-optimized IGZO arrays is proposed for the heterogeneous CIM/CNM architecture. Experiment results illustrate that the 3DIG-CXM achieves 25.9× area savings compared with the SRAM CIM baseline, and 1.2×∓3.0× energy efficiency on the encoder/decoder compared to the off-chip HBM baseline.

论文题名

Event-triggered visual neuron based on memristor for privacy-preserving and efficient neuromorphic sensing

期刊：Science China Information Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11432-025-4952-9 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Biological visual neurons encode changes in light intensity through joint modulation of spike polarity and frequency, enabling efficient detection of dynamic events and significantly reducing sensory data redundancy. However, existing neuromorphic sensors with the biomimetic neuron spiking characteristics are still lacking. Here, we propose an event-triggered visual neuron that combines light sensing and frequency-based encoding in a compact structure. This architecture maps biological neuron behavior onto hardware, producing bipolar spike trains with frequencies ranging from 58.3 to 191.4 kHz that reflect luminance variation, with a dynamic range over 100 dB. Unlike conventional sensors, the event-triggered visual neuron circuit directly encodes changes into frequency-modulated pulses, mimicking retinal dynamics and suppressing static leakage. Experiments show event-triggered visual neuron outputs resist deep learning-based privacy attacks: recognition accuracy on event data drops to chance levels, while remaining high on original videos. We further developed a neuromorphic pose estimation framework based on event-triggered visual neuron output, achieving keypoint recognition accuracy comparable to conventional frame-based methods while significantly reducing power consumption and data bandwidth requirements.
