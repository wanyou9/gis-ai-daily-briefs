论文题名

Modelling under-reported data: pitfalls of naïve approaches and a new statistical framework for epidemic curve reconstruction

期刊：Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series A: Statistics in Society · DOI：10.1093/jrsssa/qnag106 · 摘要来源：账本已存摘要

Count-valued autoregressions are widely used to analyse time-series of reported infectious-disease cases because of their close connection with discrete-time transmission models. However, when such models are applied directly to under-reported case counts, their mechanistic interpretation can break down. We establish new theoretical results quantifying the consequences of ignoring under-reporting in these models. To address this issue, reported cases are often modelled as a binomially thinned version of an underlying count process, but such models are difficult to fit because the unobserved true counts are serially correlated and integer-valued. We therefore develop a new statistical framework for under-reported infectious-disease data that uses a normal–normal approximation to a broad class of thinned count autoregressions and then maps this continuous process back to the integers. Through simulations and applications to rotavirus incidence in a German state and COVID-19 incidence in English conurbations, we demonstrate that our approach both retains the mechanistic appeal of thinned autoregressions and substantially simplifies inference.

期刊：Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series A: Statistics in Society · DOI：10.1093/jrsssa/qnag107 · 摘要来源：账本已存摘要

Official statistics are undergoing a significant transformation, as national statistical institutes transition from traditional single-source data production systems to integrated systems of statistical registers combining administrative, census, and survey data. The resulting multisource register-based estimates are prone to multiple interacting sources of error, yet rigorous scalable frameworks for quantifying their accuracy remain underdeveloped. This work discusses and validates a global measure of error assessment for such multisource register-based statistics. The proposed measure is interpretable, flexible, and computationally scalable, enabling on-the-fly accuracy quantification for user-defined, unplanned domain-specific statistics on population totals. Its validity is established theoretically and confirmed through simulation studies. An application to education data from the Italian National Institute of Statistics is presented.

Hierarchical Bayesian mixture model for subgroup-specific cardiometabolic phenotypes

期刊：Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series A: Statistics in Society · DOI：10.1093/jrsssa/qnag109 · 摘要来源：账本已存摘要

Anthropometric, cardiometabolic, and renal traits—including height, adiposity, blood pressure, blood glucose, cholesterol, and kidney function—affect numerous noncommunicable and infectious diseases. These traits share some genetic, environmental and nutritional determinants while also having distinct causes. Their complex distributions and correlations further vary among population subgroups. Traditional clustering methods for multiple continuous variables often overlook subgroup-specific distributions or hinder comparisons across separately clustered subgroups. We developed a Bayesian hierarchical mixture model to coherently identify anthropometric, cardiometabolic and renal phenotypes across population subgroups. Our model balances flexibility and comparability by adapting a Bayesian hierarchical mixture model to include subgroup-specific cluster weights and empirically driven shifts in cluster means. This structure allows subgroup-specific variations on phenotype characteristics while maintaining a shared structure and interpretation. We applied this model to anthropometric, cardiometabolic and renal data of men from three different age subgroups in the US National Health and Nutrition Examination Surveys. The results demonstrate the model’s ability to identify relevant phenotypes within each age subgroup and maintain comparability across them. The model can be applied to a range of other unsupervised clustering tasks related to health, sociodemographic or environmental data that have complex patterns with similarities and differences across subgroups.

论文题名

A cylindrical Galton Board at the Galton Board’s 150th anniversary

期刊：Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series A: Statistics in Society · DOI：10.1093/jrsssa/qnag110 · 摘要来源：账本已存摘要

The celebrated Galton Board, or Quincunx, remains a widely used device for demonstrating how repeated Bernoulli trials on a triangular lattice generate an approximately normal distribution. Marking the 150th anniversary of Galton’s 1875 construction, this paper revisits the historical apparatus and extends it to a cylindrical setting in which the peg lattice is wrapped around a cylinder, enforcing angular periodicity and producing height-dependent behaviour absent from the classical design. This modification connects Galton’s original demonstration of variation and the emergence of the normal distribution with modern developments in circular statistics, providing a physical realisation of binomial random walks on a circular–linear product space. We distinguish configurations where the wrapped lattice subtends only a strict arc from those spanning the full circumference, and show how the resulting geometry yields wrapped binomial, wrapped normal, and circular uniform distributions. We describe the design and construction of our physical model, outline practical considerations for replication, and analyse the statistical properties and pedagogical applications of this cylindrical variant, which offers a modern reinterpretation of Galton’s pioneering work.

论文题名

Using shot location transitions to learn tennis player strategies

期刊：Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series A: Statistics in Society · DOI：10.1093/jrsssa/qnag112 · 摘要来源：账本已存摘要

It is common for tennis players to set up a winning shot via a calculated previous shot. To study this phenomenon we employ data from five Grand Slam matches that include Roger Federer, Rafael Nadal, Novak Djokovic, and Juan del Potro on the three major surfaces (hard court, clay, and grass) to infer shot location based on a tennis court that has been divided into six zones in a novel way. Exploratory analysis alone highlights player-specific shot location preferences based on receipt location before a given shot. However, we propose a Bayesian hierarchical model and testing scheme that allows for greater understanding of player shot location preference through posterior distributions on each shot location probability estimate. Specifically, we use a Multinomial-Dirichlet model to estimate expected shot locations along with corresponding uncertainty quantification. We also utilize simultaneous posterior probability estimation methods to perform sequential testing and identify differences in player behaviour over each surface, receipt location, and shot location.
