Cyber-physical-social system and social governance in the digital-intelligent era

期刊：中国科学：信息科学 · DOI：10.1360/ssi-2026-0207 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

The digital-intelligent era is fully dawning with the maturation and widespread adoption of technologies such as big data and artificial intelligence. The deep development of digital-intelligent society has driven a fundamental transformation in research methodologies, thereby giving rise to a social governance research paradigm centered on cyber-physical-social system (CPSS). By embedding the social dimension as an intrinsic core element, CPSS establishes deep interaction and coordination mechanisms among humans, machines, objects, and the environment, thus enabling a holistic understanding of complex social systems and fostering a paradigm shift in social governance thinking. Given the strongly coupled cyber-physical-social characteristics of digital-intelligent society under the CPSS architecture, it is imperative to adopt a systems perspective to investigate the complexity mechanisms of such a society and to develop corresponding governance models and strategies. Grounded in interdisciplinary integration, this paper starts from the strong coupling features of complex social systems in the digital-intelligent era, and constructs a theoretical analysis framework that spans from network modeling of system structures, through mechanistic analysis of structural evolution and the emergence and progression of system functions, to bottom-up optimization and regulation mechanisms. This framework aims to provide scientific theoretical underpinning for addressing challenging issues in digital-intelligent social governance, and to promote a paradigm shift from traditional subjective governance to rational scientific governance. Furthermore, this paper introduces a suite of methodological approaches—including computational social science, complex network theory, complex adaptive systems theory, multi-agent simulation, nonlinear dynamics, ACP (artificial systems, computational experiments, parallel execution) simulation, and digital-intelligent twin—that support CPSS research across data-driven modeling, mechanism analysis, and optimization of regulation strategies. Finally, three typical CPSS-based scenario applications are illustrated, including the construction of smart courts in judicial governance, the application of remote sensing technology in environmental public interest litigation, and data governance.

期刊：中国科学：信息科学 · DOI：10.1360/ssi-2026-0060 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

This study investigates the dynamic pricing problem of data products in exchange trading and proposes a dynamic pricing model based on a multi-head attention proximal policy optimization (MHA-PPO) algorithm. The model comprehensively considers data quality, historical transactions, buyer behavior, and platform mechanisms, aiming to capture changes in data value and fluctuations in market demand in real time and dynamically adjust prices accordingly to improve the efficiency of data resource allocation. In the algorithm design, a multi-head attention network is adopted to enhance the pricing model's ability to perceive complex environments; meanwhile, the Clip function is used to constrain the magnitude of policy updates, thereby improving the flexibility and stability of the training process. Through simulation experiments, this study analyzes the effects of algorithm selection, product types, transaction mechanisms, factors affecting buyer behavior, platform functional service fee rates, and the extent of platform evaluation information disclosure on pricing strategies and platform revenue, and derives long-term platform pricing decisions under different market conditions. The experimental results show that the proposed model can obtain optimal pricing strategies in complex environments, providing decision support for data trading and contributing to the development of data factor markets.

论文题名

Multi-defendant probation applicability prediction: a two-step adaptive classification method based on semantic-structural fusion and similar-case knowledge augmentation

期刊：中国科学：信息科学 · DOI：10.1360/ssi-2026-0174 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Artificial intelligence and big data technologies have increasingly been used to assist sentencing discretion and improve judicial fairness and efficiency, becoming an important direction in China's digital rule-of-law development. As an important mode of sentence execution, probation determines whether a defendant is required to serve a custodial sentence. However, existing intelligent methods for probation assistance still face several challenges. First, relevant research remains limited, and prediction accuracy needs to be further improved, especially for joint-crime cases that are prevalent in judicial practice; complex inter-dependencies among defendants and the deep coupling of sentencing factors further increase the difficulty of prediction. Second, most existing algorithms lack rigorous theoretical analysis and performance guarantees; their reliance on idealized assumptions, such as independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) data, is inconsistent with the distributional characteristics of real-world judicial data, making it difficult to support reliable decision-making in legal settings. To address these challenges, this paper formulates multi-defendant probation applicability prediction as a multi-output problem under quantized observations and proposes a weighted-least-squares-based two-step adaptive classification algorithm. The proposed algorithm avoids complex projection computations, thereby improving the computational efficiency of large-scale judicial data processing. It guarantees global convergence without relying on stringent assumptions such as i.i.d. data and is therefore better aligned with the actual distributional characteristics of judicial data. Moreover, the designed adaptive predictor achieves favorable prediction performance without imposing any excitation condition on the data. In addition, we propose a hierarchical semantic-structural fusion and similar-case knowledge augmentation encoding strategy. By integrating global case semantics with defendant-specific conduct and characteristics and incorporating a temporally constrained similar-case knowledge augmentation mechanism, the proposed strategy effectively addresses the deep coupling of sentencing factors in multi-defendant scenarios and provides more comprehensive input representations for downstream prediction tasks. Experiments on probation applicability prediction using real-world multi-defendant judgment data demonstrate that, compared with mainstream baseline models, the proposed method not only improves prediction accuracy but also enhances computational efficiency, validating its effectiveness in judicial data settings.

Multi-agent evolutionary game and synergistic governance of emergency events: a CPSS-based approach

期刊：中国科学：信息科学 · DOI：10.1360/ssi-2026-0230 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

In the digital era, information governance of emergencies exhibits complex coupling characteristics within the cyber-physical-social system (CPSS). Traditional static perspectives struggle to reveal the dynamic evolution laws governing the behaviors of multiple stakeholders, including the government, information platforms, and the public. This paper constructs a tripartite evolutionary game model under the CPSS framework, introduces artificial intelligence technology maturity and the rumor propagation coefficient, and analyzes the nonlinear regulatory effect of the information layer on strategy evolution. Using evolutionary stability analysis and numerical simulation, the evolutionarily stable strategies under different scenarios are identified. The research indicates that, first, different types of emergencies will endogenously drive the system to converge to differentiated evolutionarily stable strategies by changing the intensity of information propagation, governance costs, and the stakeholder benefit structure, showing significant scenario dependence. Second, the maturity of artificial intelligence technology presents a threshold-type promotion effect: when technology maturity is below the critical threshold, the system oscillates and loses stability; once it crosses the threshold, the higher its maturity, the faster and more stably the system converges to the efficient collaborative steady state. Third, the influence of rumor propagation intensity exhibits a nonlinear characteristic of low stability, medium disturbance, and high failure. Once the rumor intensity breaks through the critical threshold, it is sufficient to trigger a cliff-like failure of the platform's governance capacity, destroying the original collaboration. Instead, high-intensity rumors give rise to a new asymmetric steady state characterized by strong government governance, continuous passivity of platforms, and public rationality. From the CPSS perspective, this study reveals the internal mechanism ofcollaborative emergency governance, and provides a theoretical reference for digital emergency governance.

论文题名

Componentwise adaptive prediction theory under saturated observations and its application to stage-coupled sentencing

期刊：中国科学：信息科学 · DOI：10.1360/ssi-2026-0235 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Judicial sentencing prediction often faces challenges arising from statutory sentencing-range constraints, high-dimensional heterogeneous features, and non-Gaussian disturbances. To address these issues, this paper studies the adaptive prediction problem for a class of stochastic dynamic models with time-varying saturation mappings, and proposes a componentwise adaptive likelihood gradient algorithm (CALG) based on the Laplace conditional negative log-likelihood. CALG adaptively updates different components according to the historical information along each coordinate, making it suitable for high-dimensional, sparse, and heterogeneous structured sentencing data. Theoretically, this paper establishes a cumulative regret upper bound for CALG, and shows that, under commonly used sentencing evaluation metrics, the adaptive predictor generated by CALG can asymptotically achieve the same performance as the Oracle predictor with the true parameter. To verify the applicability of the proposed theoretical framework to real judicial scenarios, this paper constructs a stage-coupled sentencing model for theft cases, consisting of fixed-term imprisonment prediction, probation applicability determination, and probation prediction. The model formulates fixed-term imprisonment and probation prediction as stochastic regression problems with time-varying saturation mappings, and formulates probation applicability determination as a binary classification problem jointly governed by a principal-sentence threshold and a case-level score, thereby explicitly capturing statutory range constraints and the staged logic of judicial sentencing. On real theft judgment data, residual-distribution fitting and scale-parameter estimation experiments are conducted to examine the empirical plausibility of the Laplace noise assumption. Subsequently, in the three-stage prediction tasks, CALG is compared with Newton-like adaptive methods for saturation-observation models, non-componentwise updating methods, commonly used first-order optimizers, and large-language-model evaluation baselines. The experimental results show that the proposed method achieves overall better predictive accuracy and computational efficiency in fixed-term imprisonment prediction, probation applicability determination, and probation prediction.

论文题名

A reinforcement learning-driven method for intelligent harmful information detection

期刊：中国科学：信息科学 · DOI：10.1360/ssi-2026-0335 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Harmful information detection is of great significance for maintaining the security of the online ecosystem and preventing social risks, and constitutes a key task in digitalized social governance. In recent years, harmful information detection methods based on artificial intelligence, especially large language models, have made substantial progress in automated identification. Meanwhile, in real-world online environments where implicit expressions frequently emerge and internet slang continues to evolve, incorporating external semantic information can help supplement models' understanding of contextual semantics and implicit harmful intent, thereby further improving harmful information detection performance. However, existing external knowledge-based semantic enhancement methods mostly rely on static matching or rule-triggered retrieval, making it difficult for models to adaptively determine whether external knowledge is necessary and what specific knowledge should be introduced. This may introduce semantic noise and limit the discriminative performance and generalization ability of the model. To address these issues, this paper proposes an active lexicon retrieval method based on counterfactual rewards. The proposed method formulates the external knowledge retrieval process as a policy learning problem. Through reinforcement learning, the model adaptively decides whether to retrieve external knowledge and which key semantic terms to retrieve. Moreover, a counterfactual retrieval gain reward mechanism is designed to guide the model toward high-value retrieval behaviors that make actual contributions to the discrimination task, thereby enabling a shift from a rule-driven retrieval paradigm to a semantic-driven retrieval paradigm. Experimental results on SWSR, ToxiCN, and STATE-ToxiCN, which exhibit varying levels of linguistic complexity and different distributions of implicit harmful expressions, demonstrate that the proposed method consistently improves performance across different detection tasks, data distributions, and model scales (3B and 7B). The improvements are particularly pronounced in scenarios with a higher prevalence of metaphorical expressions and internet slang, validating the effectiveness and generalizability of the proposed method in complex online semantic environments.

A multi-task multimodal reasoning framework for oracle bone character interpretation

期刊：中国科学：信息科学 · DOI：10.1360/ssi-2025-0551 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Oracle bone script decipherment is a crucial step toward explaining the origin of Chinese characters and advancing the study of Chinese civilization. However, nearly two-thirds of oracle bone glyphs still lack reliable decipherment. Existing artificial intelligence-based methods are mostly limited to glyph matching and local feature analysis. They lack the ability to integrate evidence across historical periods and therefore struggle to construct systematic reasoning chains that conform to philological logic. To address these issues, this paper proposes ORACLE-PRIME, a multi-task joint, three-stage progressive multimodal reasoning framework for single-character semantic interpretation of oracle bone script. On the one hand, it jointly trains tasks such as glyph perception, structural periodization, and evolutionary reasoning to achieve multi-level evolutionary analysis of oracle bone characters. On the other hand, it introduces a multi-period glyph classification module and rule-based reward functions to reduce the discrepancy between semantic interpretation results and the evolutionary analysis process. To support this training paradigm, this paper constructs the first 400k-scale pretraining corpus covering font libraries, radical decomposition, evolutionary images, and authoritative interpretations. It further proposes M3T-OBC, a manually verified high-quality reasoning dataset containing 9116 samples, to evaluate models' ability to perform multi-period single-character reasoning. Compared with the baseline model Qwen2.5-VL-7B and several of its training variants, the proposed method achieves superior category accuracy and reasoning-text consistency in both deciphered and simulated undeciphered scenarios, and outperforms various open-source and closed-source multimodal models on PictOBI-20k and OBIBench. Furthermore, on multiple simulated undeciphered characters, the proposed method can generate detailed and correct multi-stage evolutionary reasoning results, providing more sufficient auxiliary evidence for oracle bone script interpretation.

Experimental results show that under 5 subcarriers and an SNR of 17 dB, the ITC algorithm achieves 100% demodulation accuracy.
