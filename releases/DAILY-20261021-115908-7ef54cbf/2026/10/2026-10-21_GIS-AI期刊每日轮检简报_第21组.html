论文题名

A new perspective on centroid algorithms for unsupervised discrete clustering in Riemannian manifolds: an approach via optimization

期刊：Soft Computing · DOI：10.1007/s00500-026-11422-8 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

Clustering is an unsupervised learning technique used to discover hidden structures in data. By identifying patterns and similarities among objects, this methodology enables the organization of datasets into homogeneous groups. This work presents a new perspective on a specific class of partition-based algorithms for unsupervised discrete clustering in complete finite-dimensional Riemannian manifolds by introducing statistical shape analyses to classify clusters and improve label selection. To achieve this aim, we extend the statistical concepts of skewness and kurtosis to Riemannian settings, based on vector operations in the tangent plane at each point of the manifold. Assumptions regarding the injectivity radius and the boundedness of the sectional curvature are initially adopted to enable local convex analysis. Nevertheless, the well-posedness of Riemannian weighted centroids is ensured by analyzing the coercivity and quasiconvexity properties of the distance function’s powers. In addition, we demonstrate that well-posedness and continuity extend to any positive power of the distance function, rather than being limited to powers greater than or equal to 1. Computational experiments involving diffusion tensor imaging and hyperspectral image segmentation are performed. We also present a statistical analysis to demonstrate the practical applicability and computational performance of our technique compared with leading clustering approaches. Ultimately, our methodology is applicable to any field where data modeling resides in a complete finite-dimensional Riemannian manifold.

摘要（可展开，供朗读/NotebookLM）

论文题名

Sequential and Decoupled Reliability Analysis applied to time-dependent engineering systems

期刊：Soft Computing · DOI：10.1007/s00500-026-11486-6 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

Traditionally, reliability analysis neglects the influence of time, leading to inaccurate predictions for systems under progressive degradation and variable loads. However, addressing time dependency is of major significance, as material aging and cumulative damage dynamically alter the system’s condition, increasing the probability of failure throughout its operational lifespan. Time-dependent (or time-variant) reliability analysis addresses this challenge, as time directly affects failure probability. This paper proposes and evaluates two methodologies: Sequential Reliability Analysis (SRA) and Decoupled Reliability Analysis (DRA). SRA transforms the time-dependent problem into an equivalent time-independent one by discretizing time, using a metamodel to approximate the failure probability profile, and performing a one-dimensional search for the optimal value. In this method, reliability analysis is required at each discretized time point. Conversely, DRA also removes time dependency but does so by replacing the time variable with a metamodel dependent on random variables, requiring only one reliability analysis. Both methodologies were validated through mathematical and engineering case studies. The results demonstrate that both provide accurate failure probability estimates, with errors below 2% when compared to reference solutions or Monte Carlo Method (MCM) results. Additionally, the DRA approach achieved a computational cost reduction of up to 99.8% relative to the traditional method. These findings confirm the effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed approaches for time-dependent reliability analysis in engineering applications.

论文题名

Consider stock indices and technical indicators to conduct price forecasting for gold and oil: a reinforcement learning perspective

期刊：Soft Computing · DOI：10.1007/s00500-026-11554-x · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

Rising oil prices often trigger inflation, whereas higher gold prices reflect risk aversion. Analyzing daily data from 2011 to 2025, this study proposes a novel framework to predict commodity prices: (1) raw materials, stock indices, and technical indicators are integrated as the predictors, (2) key performance indicators (KPIs) are identified using MARS (multivariate adaptive regression splines), RF (Random Forest), and XGB (eXtreme gradient boost), and (3) machine learning (ML), deep learning (DL) models including LSTM (long short-term memory) and GRU (gated recurrent unit), and reinforcement learning (RL) strategies such as A2C (advantage actor–critic) and PPO (proximal policy optimization) are compared. Specifically, one-shot splitting starts from 2011 to 2022 for training and uses 2023–2025 is for testing. In dynamic splitting, four-year training and one-month testing is adopted. Results show that average exchange rate of US dollar (USD), coal, India’s stock, Shanghai’s stock, US10YY (10-year yield rate of US treasury bond), Japan’s stock, DJI (US Dow Jones index), and NASDAQ (US technology index) affect oil prices while silver, S&P 500, German’s stock, SOX (US semiconductor index), India’s stock, and NASDAQ influence gold prices. Dynamic splitting and RL models outperform one-shot splitting because they can capture short-term variations and abrupt changes.
