期刊：地理学报 · DOI：10.11821/dlxb202610001 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

在全球科技竞争与大国博弈交织的背景下，高层次科研人才作为国家创新系统的核心战略资源，其竞争优势的结构性差异深刻影响着全球科技竞争格局。本文基于知识生产视角，构建“规模—效率—结构—绩效”（SESP）四维分析框架，将产品空间理论中的遍在性、多样性和复杂性纳入国家间人才竞争优势的解构中，揭示国家间高层次科研人才竞争优势的结构性差异及其驱动机制。研究发现：① 全球高层次科研人才及其知识生产呈现显著的空间极化特征，美国、中国、英国、德国等15国占据全球90%以上的份额，且高度集中于医学、理学和工学等“巴斯德象限”学科领域，形成“中心—边缘”梯度格局；② 人才及其知识生产的遍在性、多样性与复杂性存在显著关联，低遍在性学科领域多具有高技术壁垒或区域禀赋依赖性，而高遍在性学科则呈现基础性和通用性特征，人才多样性与平均遍在性呈负相关，高多样性国家更易培养和集聚稀缺人才并生产复杂性知识；③ 国家人才竞争优势呈现“金字塔”分层，美国凭借规模与绩效优势占据主导，中国以结构见长但效率与绩效不足，德英等国则依托效率—结构平衡维持竞争力；④ 教育发展和经济水平是驱动人才竞争优势的核心因子，且与创新环境、对外开放水平等交互作用，形成非线性增强效应。研究有助于突破“规模决定论”或“产出决定论”的局限，捕捉人才竞争优势的复杂特征，弥补现有研究对人才结构维度的忽视，为国家优化人才结构、提升知识生产效能提供学理参考。

基于2016—2024年中国上市企业供应商—客户企业数据，构建了城市间竞合网络模型，并运用社会网络分析方法与双重机器学习模型，分析了中国城市竞合网络的时空特征及其对城市创新的影响。研究发现：① 基于企业供应链的城市竞合网络可划分为垂直协同型合作网络（VSCN）、垂直竞争型合作网络（VCCN）、水平协同型合作网络（HSCON）和水平协同型竞争网络（HSCN）4种子网络。研究期内，中国城市竞合网络呈现出“结构持续优化、受地理距离影响明显和子网络差异显著”的总体结构特征。② 4类子网络节点联系水平呈现出相似性与差异性的统一，整体均表现为“层级性显著、极化特征突出和空间分异明显”的分布格局，但相比于垂直型网络，水平型网络的空间极化程度和层级结构更为显著。③ VSCN联系与VCCN联系能够显著促进中国城市创新发展，且VSCN联系的促进作用更为突出，但二者对城市创新的影响均呈现边际效应递减的特征；HSCON联系仅对城市群内城市创新具有显著的促进作用，HSCN联系对城市创新的影响具有非线性的倒“U”型特征。④ 不同的空间尺度、联系方向和地理区位，城市竞合网络联系对城市创新的影响存在异质性。研究结果丰富了对城市间竞合理论的理解，并为促进区域协同创新提供了实证基础与政策参考。

在数字中国建设与区域协调发展战略背景下，政府数字采购日益成为推动城市间创新要素跨域流动与数字创新网络重构的重要制度工具。本文基于2017—2022年政府采购合同与专利合作数据，构建中国城市间政府数字采购网络与数字创新网络，采用多层复杂网络分析与指数随机图模型（ERGM），探讨政府数字采购嵌入对数字创新网络结构演化的影响及其异质化作用机制。研究发现：中国城市间政府数字采购网络规模持续扩大，逐步形成长三角、珠三角、陕甘宁等多中心组团格局；数字创新网络同步呈现去中心化的演化趋势，其结构性行为与采购网络高度趋同。政府数字采购网络对创新网络具有显著的嵌入协同作用，跨城市采购均可提升城市间数字合作创新的潜力。此外，城市经济水平、数字基础设施与数字企业集聚度构成数字创新网络嵌入的关键支撑，多维邻近性显著提升了跨城市数字创新的合作概率，城市群与地区间数字创新机制呈现空间分化特征。可见，政府数字采购不仅是公共服务获取机制，更是具有结构性嵌入功能的网络型政策工具，对中小城市实现制度化创新嵌入与区域创新格局优化具有重要意义。本文为理解政府政策如何通过制度网络重塑数字创新空间结构提供了新的理论视角与实证支撑。

Low-altitude information networks (LAINs), as the key infrastructure supporting these activities, are required not only to ensure reliable communication, but also to support environmental sensing and target identification. Existing research on integrated sensing and communications (ISAC) has established an important foundation for the joint design of communication and sensing functions in LAINs. This paper presents a comprehensive review of integrated sensing, communications, and identification (ISCAI) for LAE. We first provide an overview of LAINs by reviewing approaches to low-altitude communication, sensing, and identification, and explain how representative application scenarios motivate their integration. We further present the system and signal models of the resulting integrated sensing and identification (ISAI) and ISCAI paradigms, along with their multifunctional receiver designs and mutual integration gains. Finally, we outline important open problems and future directions, including the information-theoretic framework of ISCAI, artificial intelligence (AI)-empowered ISCAI system design, and reconfigurable intelligent surface-enhanced ISCAI. This paper aims to provide a structured perspective on how LAINs are evolving from the joint design of communication and sensing toward a broader integration of communication, sensing, and identification.

Decoding the heterogeneity of biological neural systems is key to understanding the nervous system’s complex dynamical behaviors. This study analyzes the comprehensive Drosophila brain connectome, which is the most recent dataset, containing over 130000 neurons and 50M synapses. We conducted meticulous analyses of both network and spatial structure. Our findings reveal significant heterogeneity in network properties and distinct spatial clustering across functional regions. Besides, our analysis revealed a modular organizational pattern within the neural network, wherein regions with similar functions exhibited higher connection densities, forming distinct community structures. Moreover, we observed spatial clustering within functional regions, but it was not statistically significant. Additionally, we identified pervasive bilateral symmetry in network topology and spatial organization. Simulations based on the Kuramoto model demonstrate that the functional asymmetry between cerebral hemispheres predominantly arises from disparities in the intrinsic frequencies of neurons rather than from structural asymmetry within the neural network itself. These insights advance our understanding of neural network organization and complexity in biological systems.

论文题名

Effects of multivariate recovery dependency on resilience and scaling laws in networks with time delays

期刊：Science China Information Sciences · DOI：10.1007/s11432-026-5233-8 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Real-world recovery processes in interdependent systems are rarely instantaneous and often involve time delays, yet their impact on network-level resilience remains poorly understood. We propose a dynamical recovery model on interdependent networks in which node-level recovery depends on both intra-layer and inter-layer support and is activated only after a finite delay. Combining mean-field analysis with large-scale simulations, we examine the long-term recovery outcomes under varying levels of initial damage and recovery delays. We identify two distinct recovery thresholds that partition the system into fully recoverable, partially recoverable, and unrecoverable regimes, corresponding to different basins of attraction in the recovery dynamics. While increasing recovery delays substantially reduces both the probability and the speed of successful recovery, the ultimate collapse threshold is governed primarily by network structure. In contrast, the lower recovery threshold is sensitive to both network size and recovery delay, whereas the upper threshold remains robust to these factors. As a result, the separation between the two thresholds follows a scaling law with network size and increases linearly with the recovery delay. Our results highlight the critical role of timely interventions in enhancing resilience and provide insights for designing recovery strategies in coupled infrastructures.
