针对监控视频难以与实景三维模型精准地理映射的问题，该文提出了一种顾及实景三维模型与梯度下降的地理映射方法。首先，基于已标定内部参数的监控视频，依托实景三维模型选取控制点，通过PnP算法进行摄像机外部参数的解算，并基于梯度下降的数值优化策略对相机外部参数进行迭代优化。然后，针对重投影误差较大的区域或者目标，引入分块策略，构建监控视频与实景三维模型视图的单应矩阵，分块进行单应变换，从而有效降低局部对齐误差，达到较好的地理映射效果。实验结果表明：(1)PnP与梯度下降协同的优化方法，对1080P监控视频，平均重投影误差<18个像素，最大重投影误差<41个像素，均方根误差<6个像素。(2)基于地理映射参数及分块单应后的视频帧，将监控视频转换为彩色点云，其与实景三维模型实现了像素级融合效果。该方法可为智慧城市、应急救灾、自然资源监测等领域的实景三维建设提供参考。

Multi-layer cube development has become a key strategy to increase the degree of regional reserve utilization and recovery rate of shale gas reservoirs in the Sichuan Basin. However, as well spacing decreases and cube well placement is promoted, both longitudinal and transversal well spacing are significantly reduced. Such reduction in spacing leads to frequent inter-well interference. This paper focuses on the inter-well interference in the first cube development platform in the medium-deep of South Sichuan, establishing a discrete fracture network-finite element method (DFN-FEM) coupled multi-field numerical model to simulate fracturing in the C1 dual-layer cube development platform. The model is validated using production test data to investigate the complex inter-well interference mechanisms. This work provides a quantitative framework for optimizing shale gas cube development in structurally complex basins.
