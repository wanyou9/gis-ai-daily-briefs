论文题名

Exploropleth: exploratory analysis of data binning methods in choropleth maps

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2025.2543483 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

When creating choropleth maps, mapmakers often bin (i.e. group, classify) quantitative data values into groups to help show that certain areas fall within a similar range of values. For instance, a mapmaker may divide counties into groups of high, middle, and low life expectancy (measured in years). It is well known that different binning methods (e.g. natural breaks, quantiles) yield different groupings, meaning the same data can be presented differently depending on how it is divided into bins. To help guide a wide variety of users, we present a new, open-source, web-based, geospatial visualization tool, Exploropleth, that lets users interact with a catalog of established data binning methods, and subsequently compare, customize, and export custom maps. This tool advances the state of the art by providing multiple binning methods in one view and supporting administrative unit reclassification on-the-fly. We interviewed 16 cartographers and geographic information systems (GIS) experts from 13 government organizations, non-government organizations (NGOs), and federal agencies who identified opportunities to integrate Exploropleth into their existing mapmaking workflow, and found that the tool has the potential to educate students as well as mapmakers with varying levels of experience. Exploropleth is open-source and publicly available at https://exploropleth.github.io.

论文题名

Reproducible visualization strategies for spatially varying coefficient (SVC) models: incorporating uncertainty and assessing replicability

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2025.2557949 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Spatially varying coefficient (SVC) models are often regarded as “big models” due to the extensive volume of outputs that they produce. This can make it challenging to identify important trends, and maps are typically used when interpreting results from these models. However, visualization best practices are often overlooked and uncertainty is not incorporated, leading to misinterpretation and complicating pattern extraction and comparison. This has important implications for reproducibility and replicability (R&R) in SVC models, which has received limited attention in the literature. Addressing these gaps requires a structured approach that enhances interpretability, facilitates model comparison, and effectively incorporates model uncertainty when analyzing SVC model output. This study introduces svc-viz, an open-source Python tool that codifies best practices into a standardized framework for interpreting and communicating SVC model results. By integrating established visualization principles, svc-viz improves clarity and reduces the risk of misinterpretation. Additionally, svc-viz introduces strategies for visualizing model uncertainty and assessing replicability across datasets, methods, and model inputs. The utility of the tool is demonstrated using a 2020 U.S. presidential election dataset. By formalizing visualization strategies, this study advances reproducibility, replicability, and uncertainty consideration in multiscale local modeling, providing researchers with a more robust framework for analyzing and communicating spatial relationships.

Optimizing electric vehicle supply equipment allocation through GIS-Based multi-criteria decision analysis in an urban environment

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2025.2571790 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

The rapid adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) presents critical infrastructure challenges, particularly in the strategic deployment of electric vehicle supply equipment (EVSE). In this study, we apply a GIS-based Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) framework to optimize EVSE allocation in an urban setting, using Philadelphia as an example. While this framework provides a structured approach to complex spatial decision-making, it is also subject to methodological uncertainties, including sensitivity to weighting schemes and the scale of the spatial unit selection. These challenges are further compounded for decision-makers who are not experts in MCDA methodologies. Through detailing the workflow of MCDA and conducting a comparative analysis of different methods and parameter choices, we assess how these decisions influence site suitability outcomes and decision reliability. Our goal is not only to support the deployment of EVSE in Philadelphia but also to highlight the strengths and limitations of GIS-MCDA in smart city planning. We offer practical insights for planners, policymakers, and researchers seeking to enhance the transparency, replicability, and effectiveness of GIS-MCDA applications. Our results indicate that methodological differences become particularly pronounced when alternatives have very similar overall scores across multiple criteria, resulting in greater variability in rankings. Additionally, extreme values in the criteria can further impact ranking stability. Uncertainties and challenges arise in the selection of criteria and weighting schemes, propagating through the process of criteria aggregation and parameter definition. A multi-scale approach that integrates multiple methods would enable a more balanced and resilient EVSE deployment strategy in Philadelphia.

While extensive research in various disciplines has revealed the institutional hierarchies inherent in these networks, their impacts within GIScience remain underexplored. 论文题名

Transitions in dynamic point labeling

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2025.2555426 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

The labeling of point features on a map is a well-studied topic. In a static setting, the goal is to find a non-overlapping label placement for (a subset of) point features. In a dynamic setting, the set of point features and their corresponding labels change, and the labeling has to adapt to such changes. To aid the user in tracking these changes, we can use morphs, here called transitions, to indicate how a labeling changes. Such transitions have not gained much attention yet, and we investigate different types of transitions for labelings of points, most notably consecutive transitions and simultaneous transitions. We give (tight) upper bounds on the number of overlaps that can occur during these transitions. When each label has a non-negative weight associated to it, and each overlap imposes a penalty proportional to the weight of the overlapping labels, we show that it is NP-complete to decide whether the penalty during a simultaneous transition has weight at most k. Finally, we consider geotagged data on a map, by labeling points with rectangular or square labels. We developed a prototype implementation to evaluate different transition styles in practice, measuring both number of overlaps and transition duration.

Mapping with a sense of place: a crowdsourced image-based color generation approach

期刊：Cartography and Geographic Information Science · DOI：10.1080/15230406.2025.2580432 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Maps are crucial for conveying the character and atmosphere of places. While maps that evoke a sense of place are increasingly recognized as important, existing methods often rely on subjective design choices, which can be inefficient and lack scalability. This paper proposes a method for generating place-aware colored maps using crowdsourced images to realize mapping with a sense of place. Feature colors of place extracted from these images are used to create colored maps that balance legibility, harmony, and imageability. To evaluate the method’s effectiveness, we conducted two comparative experiments: one comparing place-aware colored maps with Google Maps, and another with an aesthetically rich baseline – the “Hopper” style from Snazzy Maps. Evaluation was based on participants’ performance in map-reading tasks and their perceived sense of place. Results show that our colored maps perform similarly to Google Maps in map-reading tasks but significantly outperform both baselines in evoking a stronger sense of place. This indicates that place-aware colored maps effectively evoke a sense of place while maintaining basic map functionality. This research introduces a framework for mapping with a sense of place, offering a new approach to color generation that enhances user engagement through intuitive connections to place characteristics.

期刊：中国图象图形学报 · DOI：10.11834/jig.250591 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

目的遥感图像字幕生成（remote sensing image captioning， RSIC）作为融合计算机视觉与自然语言处理的跨模态任务，能将遥感图像中的关键地物、场景及关联关系转化为通俗文本，在灾害应急、农业监测等领域具有重要应用价值。然而，现有RSIC方法存在特征提取不全面如忽视多尺度纹理特征或单一语义特征依赖、跨模态信息对齐不足的问题。为此，提出一种集成多模块的RSIC新方法。方法在特征提取阶段，设计多尺度特征融合模块（multi-scale feature fusion module， MSFFM）与混合语义融合模块（hybrid semantic integration module， HSIM）：MSFFM通过结合空间—通道注意力及频域增强，强化纹理与结构信息；HSIM利用局部特征、全局语义特征，经特征维度对齐与自注意力融合，丰富图像语义表达。在文本生成阶段，构建跨模态注意力模块，融合LSTM（long short-term memory ）隐状态与单词嵌入向量生成引导信号，实现视觉—文本特征深度对齐；同时设计跨模态特征对齐损失，以温度缩放交叉熵最小化图像与文本特征语义差异。结果实验在UCM-Captions、RSICD及自建SAR-Captions数据集上验证，所提方法在BLEU1-BLEU4（bilingual evaluation understudy）、METEOR（metric for evaluation of translation with explicit ordering）、ROUGE（recall-oriented understudy for gisting evaluation）、CIDEr（consensus-based image description evaluation）指标上均优于对比模型。其中，在SAR-Captions数据集上，BLEU4值达到 0.857 0、CIDEr值达到4.260 6，较基线模型提升显著；消融实验表明MSFFM、HSIM及跨模态对齐模块对性能提升的关键作用。结论所提出的RSIC模型，能有效整合多源特征、优化跨模态对齐，为遥感图像（尤其是SAR图像）的语义解读提供高效解决方案。本文构建的人工标注SAR-Captions 图像字幕数据集已部分开源至https://github.com/hhhyrt/SAR-image-caption-datasets。

论文题名

A dual pipeline machine learning framework for automated multi class sleep disorder screening using hybrid resampling and ensemble learning

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01651-y · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Accurate classification of sleep disorder related categories, particularly insomnia and sleep apnea, remains an important machine learning research problem. This paper presents a Dual Pipeline Machine Learning Framework for multi class sleep disorder classification using the publicly available Sleep Health and Lifestyle dataset, which is described by its source as synthetic and created for illustrative purposes. The framework evaluates two alternative feature processing strategies: a statistical pipeline utilizing RobustScaler, Mutual Information, and Linear Discriminant Analysis, and a wrapper based pipeline utilizing MinMaxScaler, Boruta feature selection, and an Autoencoder for nonlinear representation learning. SMOTETomek is evaluated to address class imbalance, and nine classifiers are compared through baseline, optimized, and ablation configurations. The dataset was partitioned before learned preprocessing into a 299 sample training set and a permanently held out 75 sample test set. Stratified 8 Fold Cross Validation was used within the training partition for hyperparameter selection, while the final reported metrics were calculated exclusively on the held out test partition. K Nearest Neighbors, XGBoost, LightGBM, and Extra Trees achieved the highest observed test Accuracy of 98.67%, corresponding to 74 correct predictions among 75 test samples, with a 95% Wilson score interval of 92.83% to 99.76%. The results indicate that the effects of normalization, feature selection, and hybrid resampling are strongly classifier dependent. Macro AUC produced a different ranking from accuracy, indicating that single partition accuracy differences of one sample should not be used to order configurations. These findings should be interpreted as methodological evidence from a small synthetic tabular benchmark and do not establish clinical validity, diagnostic reliability, or readiness for real world screening.

Political stance detection on X during the 2024 U.S. election: a comparative study of classical, neural, and transformer models

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01656-7 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

As online social networks (OSNs) play an increasingly influential role in political communication, accurately detecting political stance from noisy, short-form content remains a significant challenge in computational social science (CSS). This paper presents a controlled comparison of text classification models for political stance detection during the 2024 U.S. presidential election, focusing on candidate affiliation toward Donald Trump and Joe Biden–Kamala Harris. Using nearly 100,000 posts from X, we define candidate affiliation as a three-class target variable (Trump, Biden–Kamala, or Neutral) and use topic modeling to support keyword development and thematic characterization. Under a unified preprocessing, labeling, and evaluation pipeline, we compare classical machine learning models, sentence-embedding-based neural architectures, and a domain-adapted transformer. Results suggest that task-aligned text representation accounts for most of the predictive signal in this setting. While fine-tuned BERTweet achieves the strongest in-domain performance, carefully engineered classical models, particularly linear support vector machines, remain highly competitive. Multi-feature and ablation analyses further indicate that expanded metadata did not yield reliable gains beyond strong text representations. Overall, this work provides a controlled comparison of text models for candidate affiliation on a heuristically labeled corpus.

论文题名

Cognitive networks reconstruct mindsets about STEM subjects and educational contexts in high-schoolers, undergraduates, experts and their digital twins

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01659-4 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Attitudes toward STEM develop from the interaction of conceptual knowledge, educational experiences, and affect. Here we use cognitive network science to reconstruct group mindsets as behavioural forma mentis networks (BFMNs). In this case, nodes are cue words and free associations, edges are empirical associative links, and each concept is annotated with perceived valence. We analyse BFMNs from N=981 observations spanning high school students, university students, and early-career STEM experts, alongside LLM (GPT-OSS) “digital twins” (group-level synthetic counterparts, not individual-level copies), prompted to emulate comparable profiles. Focusing also on semantic neighbourhoods (“frames”) around key target concepts (e.g., STEM subjects or educational actors/places), we quantify frames in terms of valence auras, emotional profiles, network overlap (Jaccard similarity), and concreteness relative to null baselines. Across student groups, science and research are consistently framed positively, while their core quantitative subjects (mathematics and statistics) exhibit more negative and anxiety-related auras, amplified in higher math-anxiety subgroups, evidencing a STEM-science cognitive and emotional dissonance. High-anxiety frames are also less concrete than chance, suggesting more abstract and decontextualised representations of threatening quantitative domains. Human networks show greater overlap between mathematics and anxiety than GPT-OSS. The results highlight how BFMNs capture cognitive-affective signatures of mindsets towards the target domains and indicate that these synthetic counterparts approximate cultural attitudes but miss key context-sensitive, experience-based components of human educational anxiety.

论文题名

Mind Mirror: AI-powered emotional journal and mood analysis platform

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01655-8 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Digital journaling is a low-cost channel for emotional self-monitoring, but most deployed systems reduce affect to coarse positive/negative/neutral sentiment, run inference in the cloud, and expose no reasoning for their outputs — three properties that are poorly suited to sensitive reflective text. This paper presents Mind Mirror, a locally hosted emotional journaling prototype that combines fine-grained emotion classification, token-level attribution, and local-first data handling in a single deployable system. Classification is performed by a pre-trained DistilBERT GoEmotions student checkpoint that natively emits 28 labels; The application combines those outputs after the fact into six target categories—joy, sadness, anger, fear, disgust, and surprise—by applying the 27-to-6 Ekman taxonomy mapping as stated in the official GoEmotions repository, with the neutral label omitted and the distribution renormalised; since the checkpoint does not have a six-class classification head, the aggregation process is also not included. Inference is performed on the user’s own machine through a Python FastAPI service, along with a React and TypeScript front end, a Node.js and Express back end, and local JSON storage that is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The token attributions are calculated using a leave-one-out perturbation method and are shown in the interface. Three separate evaluations are carried out and kept as distinct. The version of the checkpoint that includes the production 28-to-6 aggregation is assessed at the six-class argmax stage before the confidence and entropy presentation gate, yielding 70.80% accuracy, a macro F1 of 0.5818, and a weighted F1 of 0.7032 on a deterministic subset consisting of the first 1000 eligible examples from the GoEmotions test split, eligibility being defined as the annotations corresponding to exactly one target class; this subset is not randomly chosen and is not necessarily representative of the entire filtered split. Six separately trained six-class models are compared under the same filtering conditions: a DistilBERT baseline with constraints reaches 46.50% accuracy but collapses to the majority class, while a class-weighted configuration trained on a stratified subset of 5090 examples taken from the single-target training pool using seed = 42 achieves an accuracy of 55.83% and a macro F1 score of 0.4765 on the full 3539-example single-target test split. The average observed prototype response time was about 2.2 s over ten scripted requests on a machine that had only a CPU. Furthermore, a descriptive three-day observation involving N = 60 college students recorded their self-reported emotion choices, and in this study 90.0% of the participants selected a different emotion on at least one day; the observation is purely descriptive and does not validate anything about the classifier. The findings show that it is possible to carry out locally hosted, privacy-oriented emotion analysis with token-level attribution using off-the-shelf hardware. Here, privacy refers to a feature of the architecture and not to an actual security evaluation, and there is no evaluation of the system on journal text.

论文题名

A biclique-centric exponential random graph model for common-knowledge networks

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01658-5 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Common knowledge (CK) is a social phenomenon where individuals in a group share the same information and each knows that the others share it as well, recursively. This mutual awareness enables coordinated action and gives rise to contagion dynamics that differ from standard models such as threshold and susceptible-infectious-recovered processes. In Facebook networks, bicliques are key graph substructures for generating CK because all nodes in a biclique can share information through their walls. Motivated by the need to systematically study biclique structure and CK-based contagion, we develop a biclique-centric exponential random graph model (BC-ERGM). By incorporating a qualitative biclique term, the model is designed to generate new networks with biclique-related features similar to those of an original network, providing a framework for studying CK-based contagion on the generated networks. We also introduce a structural reduction approach to make model estimation more computationally feasible for large networks. We evaluate the framework using Erdős–Rényi (ER) graphs across multiple network sizes and average-degree settings and a stochastic block model (SBM) with pronounced community structure. In the ER experiments, BC-ERGM generally reproduces the structural properties and CK-based contagion dynamics of the original networks, while structural reduction lowers computational cost. In the SBM experiment, the edge-only, edge-plus-biclique, and edge-plus-2-star models reproduce network density similarly, but BC-ERGM more closely reproduces the biclique count, biclique-size distribution, and node-participation distribution.

论文题名

Identifying content traps in recommendation networks: comparison between regular and collective influence-based focal structure analysis

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01660-x · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Content traps are structurally cohesive regions in recommendation networks characterized by high attraction and retention. This study compares two methods for identifying such structures, Focal Structure Analysis (FSA) and Collective Influence-Focal Structure Analysis (CI-FSA), to determine which better detects structurally critical content traps in YouTube recommendation networks. We construct recommendation graphs from two discourse domains (2025 U.S. tariff expansion and 2024 Taiwan presidential election), apply both methods to extract focal sets, and evaluate them using trap-intensity scores (measuring attraction and retention), structural properties (size, centrality, clustering, participation coefficient), and network resiliency metrics (connectivity loss, fragmentation). CI-FSA identifies focal sets with 14-fold lower median size than FSA, while exhibiting substantially greater structural impact: 24% higher average trap intensity, 11-fold higher median eigenvector centrality, and twice the connectivity loss per removed node. Structural analysis reveals that CI-FSA selects nodes that bridge multiple communities, whereas FSA extracts larger, community-embedded regions with lower criticality. These findings suggest that small, propagation-critical bottlenecks play an important role in the structural organization of content traps, and that CI-FSA identifies such regions more effectively than FSA. For platform design, these findings suggest that targeted interventions on approximately 20-node structural bottlenecks may be more efficient in disrupting network connectivity than interventions on larger, approximately 300-node community structures.

The physiological phenomenon of Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) elicits diverse emotional responses in women. For some, these affective responses intensify into conditions such as Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD) and Premenstrual Exacerbations (PME). The resulting labels are content-level indications that may warrant further clinical evaluation. They do not, by themselves, constitute a clinical diagnosis, particularly for conditions such as bipolar disorder and schizophrenia, which cannot be established from isolated textual expressions.

论文题名

Influence maximization in twitter mention networks: a comparative analysis of PageRank and out-degree heuristics under the Independent Cascade model

期刊：Social Network Analysis and Mining · DOI：10.1007/s13278-026-01654-9 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Identifying structurally prominent users and selecting effective seed nodes are central problems in social network analysis and influence maximization. This study analyzes a directed Twitter mention network constructed from approximately 1.6 million tweets, comprising 552,121 users and 641,497 directed edges. We characterize user positions using PageRank, in-degree, and out-degree centrality and compare PageRank-based and out-degree-based seed selection under the Independent Cascade (IC) model. The network exhibits highly skewed structural centrality patterns. Seed-set performance is evaluated through 1,000 independent Monte Carlo simulations with a uniform propagation probability of p=0.1. We report mean spread, standard deviation, 95% confidence intervals, minimum and maximum spread, and zero-additional-activation frequency. Under the adopted graph representation and IC assumptions, the out-degree-based seed set achieves a substantially larger simulated spread than the PageRank-based seed set. This difference is consistent with the diffusion mechanism because activation attempts follow outgoing mention edges, giving high-out-degree nodes more immediate opportunities to propagate. These findings are conditional on the constructed mention graph and the uniform-probability IC model. A mention edge does not necessarily represent the direction of real-world social influence, and the simulations do not incorporate temporal dynamics, content-specific effects, or empirically estimated propagation probabilities. Therefore, the results should not be interpreted as evidence that either heuristic is universally superior for real-world influence maximization. Overall, the study provides a reproducible structural and simulation-based comparison of two seed-selection heuristics while emphasizing the importance of edge interpretation, diffusion assumptions, uncertainty quantification, and empirical validation.
