基于农业产业集群的农户数字化转型机制与路径分析——以河南省临颍县辣椒产业集群为例

论文题名

A remote sensing-based surface thermal stress index for assessing urban heat dynamics in Greater Cairo

期刊：Geocarto International · DOI：10.1080/10106049.2026.2756834 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Rapid urban growth has reshaped surface thermal conditions in Greater Cairo. This study applies a Surface Thermal Stress Index (STSI) integrating normalised land surface temperature (LST), NDVI, and NDBI from Landsat 8 annual composites processed in Google Earth Engine for 2015 and 2025. Higher STSI values indicate more favourable surface conditions rather than physiological thermal comfort. Using common spatial support, fixed pooled normalisation bounds, and weights of 0.35/0.30/0.35, mean LST decreased from 33.61 to 31.62 °C, while mean STSI increased from 0.44646 to 0.48248. The lowest score class, defined by a fixed 2015 quintile cutoff, contracted from 20.00% to 5.23% of the area. Alternative-weight maps remained strongly correlated with the baseline (r = 0.984–0.999), although priority-area boundaries varied. Year-matched population overlays, MODIS comparisons, and Landsat land-cover transitions provide demographic, thermal, and land-surface context. STSI offers a reproducible framework for preliminary daytime surface screening alongside component maps and sensitivity results.

论文题名

Satellite assessment of ecological condition and associated recovery constraints in China's main ion adsorption rare earth mining landscape from 2000 to 2025

期刊：Geocarto International · DOI：10.1080/10106049.2026.2756841 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

In Ganzhou, China, ion adsorption rare earth mining sites are registered only as points, which do not show where recovery lags. We assessed ecological condition with a Remote Sensing Ecological Index adapted to mining (M-RSEI), harmonized across Landsat sensors. Mean M-RSEI rose gradually from 0.598 in 2000–2005 to 0.670 in 2020–2025, and three quarters of the cells improved. Forest fraction, soil organic carbon and slope led the environmental variables, which explained 63% of the variance under spatial cross validation. Location and spatially varying responses accounted for 44% of GeoShapley attribution. Forest fraction led at every grain up to 3 km, and averaging 100 m predictions to 5 km raised R² to 0.94 without refitting. Near sites, cells last exposed at least 16 years before 2020 remained 0.26 M-RSEI units below never exposed cells. Cells far below empirical upper envelopes are candidates for field checks and reclamation.

论文题名

Soil erosion susceptibility modeling using stacking ensemble machine learning, Kangra District, north-western Himalaya, India

期刊：Geocarto International · DOI：10.1080/10106049.2026.2737528 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

The present study aims to predict soil erosion-prone regions in Kangra District using ML algorithms; Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest (RF), XGBoost and CatBoost and their stacking ensembles. Additionally, the study also utilizes the SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) model to enhance the interpretability of the best-performing RF algorithm. For this, a comprehensive soil erosion point dataset was developed using empirical field survey integrated with visual interpretation of Google Earth historical images. The results show RF as the best-performing model, predicting 14.7% of the area into the very high susceptibility zone, followed by XGBoost (27.1%), SVM (24.4%) and CatBoost (16.4%), whereas the stacking ensemble predicted 25.2%. The SHAP analysis of the best-performing model (RF) indicates NDVI, slope and LULC as the primary governing factors influencing soil erosion in the region. This study also provides a scientific base for policy formulation and strategic planning to mitigate soil erosion in Kangra District.

论文题名

Multi-sensor fusion of Landsat and Sentinel-1 SAR for spatiotemporal land use/land cover change mapping and land surface temperature projection in Dhaka Industrial Area

期刊：Geocarto International · DOI：10.1080/10106049.2026.2700930 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Rapid urbanization in the Global South is intensifying urban heat islands and depleting water bodies, yet monsoon cloud cover limits optical monitoring. This study evaluates multi-sensor fusion of Landsat 8/9, Sentinel-1 SAR and the Global Human Settlement Layer for LULC change mapping and LST projection in Dhaka Industrial Area, Bangladesh (2015 to 2035), using Random Forest and Gradient Boosted Trees within Google Earth Engine. SAR fusion increased LULC overall accuracy to 91.7% and eliminated water commission errors (User's Accuracy: 83.3% to 100%), while contributing negligibly to LST regression (ΔRMSE = +0.003 °C). Built-up area is projected to expand by 76.1% and water bodies to decline by 68.2% by 2035, with mean LST rising 1.86°C. The GBT model achieved a spatially validated filtered RMSE of 1.711 °C, reducing systematic bias by 54% over the optical-only baseline, with direct implications for water body conservation and heat-work policy in rapidly urbanizing delta cities.
