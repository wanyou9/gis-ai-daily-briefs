论文题名

Robustness of graph self-supervised learning to real-world noise: a case study on text-driven biomedical graphs

期刊：Applied Intelligence · DOI：10.1007/s10489-026-07583-6 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Graph Self-Supervised Learning (GSSL) enables the learning of graph representations without requiring labeled data. However, existing studies have largely focused on clean, manually curated graphs. Recent advances in NLP increasingly support the automatic extraction of knowledge graphs from text, making graph data available across a wide range of domains. However, this process inevitably introduces substantial real-world noise, which remains largely unexplored since prior robustness studies typically rely on synthetic perturbations. In this paper, we present, to the best of our knowledge, the first study of GSSL robustness on text-derived knowledge graphs. First we introduce Noise-Aware Text-Driven Graph GSSL (NATD-GSSL), a unified framework that integrates automatic graph construction, graph refinement, and GSSL. We then propose a dual-graph evaluation protocol that contrasts a noisy graph automatically derived from MedMentions with a clean reference graph from the Unified Medical Language System (UMLS), aligned through a shared gold standard. Our results reveal variability in robustness across both pretext tasks and Graph Neural Network (GNN) architectures. Relation reconstruction is highly sensitive to noise and benefits from well-defined schemas, whereas feature reconstruction is considerably more robust, achieving performance comparable to clean-graph settings. Contrastive objectives are generally less affected by noise but depend strongly on alignment with downstream tasks. GNN architecture also plays a critical role: bidirectional relational message-passing designs are better suited to noisy, text-driven KGs, while unidirectional relational ones perform best on clean graphs. All code and benchmarks are publicly available at https://github.com/OthmaneKabal/MC2GAE.

论文题名

Enhancing adversarial transferability attacks via cross-distribution semantic transformation

期刊：Applied Intelligence · DOI：10.1007/s10489-026-07369-w · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Deep neural networks are vulnerable to adversarial examples, whose cross-model transferability is critical for practical black-box attacks and model security evaluations. Existing input transformation methods, though effective, perform only a finite number of sample- and category-agnostic transformations in the input space without semantic constraints, resulting in key feature corruption, insufficient transformation diversity, and excessive computational overhead. To address these drawbacks, we depart from the traditional image-level transformation paradigm and propose Cross-Distribution Semantic Transformation (CDST), a novel framework that enhances adversarial transferability via cross-distribution semantic modeling and loss optimization. CDST constructs a transfer-oriented semantic distribution and implicitly aligns source features with target semantics to generate highly transferable perturbations. Rather than relying on explicit image-level manipulations, CDST models semantic shifts in the deep feature space and approximates the effect of infinitely many transformations based on the expected loss, with a tight upper bound derived for efficient optimization. This design preserves strong semantic diversity while introducing negligible computational overhead. Extensive experiments on ImageNet, CIFAR-10, and MS COCO demonstrate that CDST consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods across cross-model, cross-architecture, and defense-bypassing black-box attack scenarios, establishing a new paradigm for semantic-aware transferable adversarial attacks.

Adaptive multi-level multi-layer self-supervised contrastive learning for drug–target heterogeneous graph interaction prediction

期刊：Applied Intelligence · DOI：10.1007/s10489-026-07591-6 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Drug–target interaction (DTI) prediction, which leverages the representations of drugs and proteins to infer their relationships, has been widely applied in both novel drug discovery and drug repositioning. It has become a prominent research focus in bioinformatics. However, the cost of DTI prediction, using experimental methods in the laboratory, is very high. As a result, researchers are increasingly turning to computer-based models for simulation experiments. While most of these methods use supervised and semi-supervised learning methods to learn the models, which needs a mass of labeled data. Sometimes obtaining labeled data can be challenging in bioinformatics. Meanwhile, drug-protein data can be abstracted into heterogeneous graphs, and the heterogeneous graph neural network is a powerful tool to process this type of data effectively—for instance, Hu et al. proposed Heterogeneous Graph Transformer to model heterogeneous graph structures via transformer-based mechanisms, laying a foundation for efficient heterogeneous graph representation learning. In this paper, we design Adaptive Multi-level Multi-layer Self-supervised Contrastive Learning for Drug–target Heterogeneous Graph Interaction Prediction (MMCDTI) model, which utilizes self-supervised contrastive learning method (e.g., the graph contrastive learning paradigm with augmentations proposed by You et al.) and heterogeneous graph neural network to predict DTI. Specifically, we first build a drug target heterogeneous graph and encode it through a hierarchical attention mechanism that combines node level and metapath level attention. Then, instead of relying on conventional graph augmentation techniques such as random node edge perturbation or feature masking, we create two complementary views, a topology graph and a semantic graph, to drive the contrastive learning process, thereby capturing richer structural and semantic relationships without any manual labels. Finally, to acquire the local node information and global graph information of the drug-target heterogeneous graph, the multi-level multi-layer self-supervised contrastive method is designed, using the output of the intermediate layer and the final layer. Meanwhile, the attention mechanism is introduced to balance these local and global loss functions adaptively. The experimental results verify the performance of MMCDTI model compared to the state-of-the-art baselines.

Heterogeneous graph structure learning via similarity perception and structure enhancement

期刊：Applied Intelligence · DOI：10.1007/s10489-026-07589-0 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

The core of heterogeneous graph structure learning lies in constructing high-quality topological structures, where the precision of structural modeling directly determines the performance of subsequent graph representation learning and downstream tasks. Existing methods predominantly rely on raw node features or shallow embeddings to compute similarities for edge pruning and topological rewiring. Such strategies are vulnerable to feature noise and cross-type distribution shifts, leading to the mistaken removal of critical semantic edges, retention of redundant noisy edges, and an inability to capture high-order complex semantic associations among multi-typed nodes. To address these challenges, we propose HGSL-SPSE, a Heterogeneous Graph Structure Learning model based on Similarity Perception and Structure Enhancement. Our model first designs a Personalized PageRank-based similarity perception mechanism that deeply fuses raw features with global topological information in heterogeneous graphs. Combined with semantic meta-paths, it constructs multi-typed structure–semantic association matrices to precisely characterize cross-type high-order semantic dependencies among nodes. Next, a multi-perspective feature-structure collaborative enhancement module bidirectionally strengthens the raw feature graph and structure–semantic graph. Subsequently, a deep structure optimization module adaptively refines the topology through noise-edge elimination and critical-edge completion. Finally, the optimized structure and enhanced features are fed into a representation learning encoder to generate highly discriminative node embeddings for downstream tasks. To validate effectiveness, we conduct node classification and unsupervised clustering experiments on four public heterogeneous graph benchmarks, comparing against eight mainstream baselines. HGSL-SPSE achieves superior results across both tasks: https://github.com/GNNETlab/HGSL-SPSE.

论文题名

A competence-based multi-mode framework for human–machine decision-making in intelligent construction

期刊：Applied Intelligence · DOI：10.1007/s10489-026-07575-6 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Intelligent construction requires coordinated execution among participants whose information and preferences differ. Artificial intelligence (AI) agents can contribute additional assessments of construction plans, creating a need to incorporate their opinions into a collective decision process with clear human responsibility. This study develops a competence-based multi-mode framework that combines preference diagnosis with cooperative feedback. Humans and AI agents submit complete evaluations of the same alternatives. A composite similarity measure distinguishes preference intensity from comparison direction, while competence evidence, directed acceptance, and preference structural quality have separate roles in opinion weighting and uptake. Human–human, human–machine, and machine–machine similarities locate disagreement. Feedback targets the most divergent human pair, combining mutual adjustment with a common human or AI reference selected relative to the human preference center. Machine assessments remain fixed, and consensus and final aggregation involve humans only. Mathematical analysis establishes conditional monotonicity of uptake, preference preservation, and within-step contraction of the selected pair’s continuous preference difference. Controlled simulations of five construction execution packages cover two batches of 180 cases and three human group sizes. In the confirmation batch, targeted feedback reduces communication by 50.39% and maximum local disagreement by 19.78% relative to random-pair cooperation. Internal and external comparisons, common-budget experiments, and sensitivity analyses characterize the coordination trade-offs. Static AI references can change human trajectories and final rankings. The framework provides an explicit route for using AI opinions in human-led construction-plan coordination.

Controller aware explainability of PPO tracking control: a mass spring damper benchmark

期刊：Applied Intelligence · DOI：10.1007/s10489-026-07553-y · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Interpreting a learned controller requires separating action attribution, closed loop behavior, and physical control structure. This study presents a controller aware diagnostic workflow for a proximal policy optimization (PPO) controller on a second order mass spring damper tracking benchmark. Existing SHAP, LIME, and Integrated Gradients methods are combined with grouped observation masking, scenario-wise rank comparisons, and finite difference sensitivities of MPC, iterative learning, admittance, and impedance baselines. The contribution is the organization and interpretation of these diagnostics, rather than a new attribution algorithm or a stability guarantee. In the reported five-training-seed, four-scenario study, PPO has the lowest aggregate RMSE (0.058± 0.050 m), but the highest RMS force (14.279± 6.466 N) and a saturation fraction of 0.034. These are descriptive results on scenario families also used for baseline tuning, not an independent generalization test or an effort matched superiority result. Feature rankings vary with the explanation method and scenario; grouped interventions cause larger tracking degradation for state and reference channels than for the explicit force channel. Redundant observations and potentially out of distribution interventions limit causal interpretation. Asymmetric sensor-noise tests are reported only as PPO observation stress, and repeated scenario seed observations do not establish independent statistical replication. The benchmark illustrates how controller aware explanations can expose tracking actuation trade offs and interpretation limits, while leaving deployment, nonlinear generalization, and formal reliability unresolved.

Boundary-aware few-shot medical image segmentation with refinement module and self-supervised learning

期刊：Applied Intelligence · DOI：10.1007/s10489-026-07590-7 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

Few-shot segmentation models have gained great popularity in the medical image segmentation community. Most existing methods often encode support and query images to feature maps, generating prototypes from support features and labels, and comparing query features to these prototypes to identify regions of interest. Photogrammetric three-dimensional (3D) mapping supports defect documentation for stone-carved heritage. However, complex surface appearance and varying view coverage hinder reliable two-dimensional (2D)–to-3D defect mapping. To address these challenges, this study proposes a local-prior–guided semiautomatic segmentation and visibility-normalized photogrammetric mapping method. First, coarse defect boxes guide region-of-interest segmentation to produce pixel-level defect masks, while category labels retain the defect class. Second, structure-from-motion and multi-view stereo (SfM-MVS) reconstruct the point cloud and recover camera geometry. The predicted masks are projected onto the 3D surface, and depth consistency removes inconsistent projections. Finally, multi-view semantic evidence is aggregated using a joint rule that combines a minimum support count with a support ratio normalized by geometrically valid views. Experiments covered multiple heritage objects at the Qianling Mausoleum and two independent public objects. On the statue test set, 2D segmentation achieved 74.21 ± 2.25% mean intersection over union (IoU) and 83.96 ± 1.80% mean Dice similarity coefficient. With the 3D decision parameters fixed on statue A, three views of statue B were excluded from fusion and used only for evaluation. The resulting macro-F1 and macro-IoU were 88.43 ± 0.24% and 84.63 ± 0.41%, respectively. Further tests trained on one statue and tested on the other and extended complete 3D mapping to the Qianling Uninscribed Stele, providing further evidence of transfer across different objects and deterioration patterns. These results indicate that local priors support fine-grained 2D defect delineation, while geometric verification and visibility-normalized multi-view aggregation support reliable 3D semantic mapping within the evaluated SfM-MVS photogrammetric setting.

Under family-wise error rate control, routine procedures such as Bonferroni, Holm, Hochberg and Hommel compare ordered p-values with fixed, rank-indexed critical constants and can leave power unused when an alternative model is credible. We study the most powerful such policy in an exchangeable product model over K hypotheses. Given the configuration, the p-values are independent, uniform under the null and drawn from a common alternative density; with the number of true effects unknown, the objective is average power weighted by a prior on it. We show that this objective and the error constraints share a closed form in elementary symmetric polynomials of the likelihood ratios, for every K and prior. Nonnegativity of the error coefficients then yields a global monotonicity theorem, reducing K coupled complementarity conditions to coordinate-wise root-finding and giving symmetric-polynomial optimal testing (SPOT), a bisection coordinate-descent algorithm with per-sweep cost polynomial in K. We also prove linear convergence under local non-degeneracy and, for fixed K, √(N)-consistency of the Monte Carlo output under the stated regularity conditions when its achieved complementarity residual is Oₚ(N^(−1/2)). A simulation study in distributional data analysis additionally shows that, compared with conventional approaches based on total in-sample variance explained, selecting a representation using CLaRe can preserve individual-level characteristics that are important for valid downstream statistical modeling. Gaussian Graphical Models for Partially Observed Multivariate Functional Data

期刊：Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics · DOI：10.1080/10618600.2026.2756975 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

In many applications, the variables that characterize a stochastic system are measured along a second dimension, such as time. This results in multivariate functional data and the interest is in describing the statistical dependencies among these variables. It is often the case that the functional data are only partially observed. This creates additional challenges to statistical inference, since the functional principal component scores, which capture all the information from these data, cannot be computed. Under an assumption of Gaussianity and of partial separability of the covariance operator, we develop an Expectation-Maximization (EM)-type algorithm for penalized inference of a functional graphical model from multivariate functional data which are only partially observed. A simulation study and an illustration on environmental, social and governance (ESG) data show the potential of the proposed method.

论文题名

Detection of mean changes in partially observed functional data

期刊：Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics · DOI：10.1080/10618600.2026.2754092 · 摘要来源：已核验来源

We propose a test for a change in the mean for a sequence of functional observations that are only partially observed on subsets of the domain, with no information available on the complement. The framework accommodates important scenarios, including both abrupt and gradual changes. The significance of the test statistic is assessed via a permutation test. In addition to the classical permutation approach with a fixed number of permutation samples, we also discuss a variant with controlled resampling risk that relies on a random (data-driven) number of permutation samples. The small sample performance of the proposed methodology is illustrated in a Monte Carlo simulation study and an application to real data.
