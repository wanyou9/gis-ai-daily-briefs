论文题名

A Landmark‐Turnbull Estimator for Non‐Markovian Illness‐Death Models With Interval‐Censored Observations

期刊：Statistical Analysis and Data Mining: The ASA Data Science Journal · DOI：10.1002/sam.70102 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

Handling interval‐censored data poses significant challenges, particularly in multi‐state models where transition times are uncertain and computational complexity is high. This study investigates the use of landmark‐based estimators tailored for interval‐censored settings, with a particular focus on three‐state illness–death models. We propose two estimators: the Landmark‐Turnbull (LMTB) estimator, which integrates the Turnbull method for nonparametric survival estimation, and the Interval Landmark (ILM) estimator, a simplified alternative that uses midpoint imputation within the landmarking framework. Both approaches allow the estimation of transition probabilities without imposing the Markov assumption, thereby offering greater flexibility and interpretability in realistic clinical settings. The performance of the proposed estimators is compared with the standard Interval Aalen–Johansen (IAJ) estimator. Simulation studies and an application to synthetic data inspired by colon cancer patient trajectories demonstrate that LMTB yields more accurate estimates for long‐term transitions, while ILM is preferable for short‐term predictions due to its lower variance and computational simplicity.

论文题名

Rapid High Utility Sequential Pattern Mining With Negative and Positive Values

期刊：Statistical Analysis and Data Mining: The ASA Data Science Journal · DOI：10.1002/sam.70107 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

High Utility Sequential Pattern Mining (HUSPM) can evaluate the practical value of patterns while capturing temporal relationships. However, negative utility items (such as discounts, returns, and wastage) prevalent in real‐world applications pose two major challenges to existing methods: Firstly, negative utility items complicate the construction of projection databases and utility calculations. Traditional structures often require multiple accesses to the original sequence, incurring high time and memory costs; secondly, existing upper bounds often prove insufficiently compact when both positive and negative utilities coexist, thereby diminishing pruning effectiveness and inducing candidate explosion. To address these issues, this paper proposes the Rapid mining High Utility Sequential Pattern with Negative items (RHUSN) algorithm. This algorithm designs a compact data structure, Neg‐Seq‐Pro, which employs a shared negative sequence array and explicit projection lists to achieve unified modeling and rapid computation of both positive and negative utilities. Concurrently, it introduces a tighter utility upper bound, NTRSU, alongside a corresponding two‐tier pruning strategy comprising ‘prefix‐level pruning’ and ‘item‐level pruning’. This approach significantly reduces candidate expansion and redundant computation while ensuring result completeness. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that RHUSN achieves notable advantages over existing representative methods in terms of runtime, memory consumption, and the number of candidate patterns. This validates the effectiveness and scalability of the proposed data structure and upper bound pruning strategy in negative utility scenarios.

论文题名

Robust Functional Basis Neural Network Method With the Normalization

期刊：Statistical Analysis and Data Mining: The ASA Data Science Journal · DOI：10.1002/sam.70110 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

This paper aims to develop a robust nonlinear model utilizing neural networks for function‐on‐function regression problems. The proposed framework is designed to enhance robustness in regression modeling by minimizing the impact of outliers in the response variable during model training. To achieve this, we introduce a robust loss function tailored to handle outliers effectively applied within a neural network based on basis expansion, and propose a novel functional normalization technique that normalizes the input function values of hidden layers with respect to time points. This approach demonstrates faster convergence as compared to existing neural network models and is less sensitive to hyperparameter settings. It also achieves high performance regression results even with outliers in the response variable. The effectiveness and superiority of the proposed method are validated through extensive simulation studies and real‐world applications using COVID‐19 excess mortality data.

论文题名

Integrative Analysis of Heterogeneous Multisource Partly Interval‐Censored Data

期刊：Statistical Analysis and Data Mining: The ASA Data Science Journal · DOI：10.1002/sam.70113 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

Modern biomedical and epidemiological studies increasingly combine data from multiple institutions, where differences in study populations and designs can induce cross‐source heterogeneity. To address this issue, we propose an integrative analysis framework for multisource partly interval‐censored data. The proposed method adopts a decomposition strategy to share information across studies while distinguishing common homogeneous effects from source‐specific heterogeneous effects. It also incorporates an MIC‐based ‐penalized procedure for tuning‐free variable selection, thereby enhancing selection stability and computational efficiency. In particular, this MIC‐based implementation avoids the joint tuning parameter search required by conventional penalties in the proposed two‐penalty framework. The estimation consistency, selection consistency, and asymptotic normality of the proposed estimators are established. Simulation studies and an application to the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative and the National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center databases demonstrate the effectiveness and practical utility of the proposed method.

论文题名

An Integrated Gradient Boosting Machine Approach With Fisher's Discriminant Analysis: Empirical Assessment on Five Benchmark Datasets and Simulations

期刊：Statistical Analysis and Data Mining: The ASA Data Science Journal · DOI：10.1002/sam.70114 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

The aim of this study is to assess the impact of different dimensionality reduction techniques—specifically Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Fisher's Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA)—on the classification performance of Gradient Boosting Machines (GBM). While PCA is commonly employed to reduce feature dimensionality, often to speed up training or lower memory usage, its application is not standard in boosting pipelines. Moreover, to the best of our knowledge, no previous work has systematically explored whether LDA can enhance performance or is compatible with gradient boosting frameworks. In addition to evaluating the effects of PCA and LDA on the predictive performance of GBM, we introduce a novel integrated iterative algorithm that incorporates LDA‐based feature transformation at each boosting step. Our analysis spans five classical benchmark datasets, representing diverse scenarios in terms of sample size, number of features, for both binary and multiclass targets. Results show that applying LDA to extract features—and especially using the proposed LDA‐integrated GBM—improves classification accuracy compared to standard GBM, particularly in multiclass settings or in binary problems with high feature dimensionality. Finally, through a set of controlled simulations, we investigate how the performance of the integrated approach evolves with increasing feature dimensionality and varying levels of feature correlation and sample size.

论文题名

Large Covariance Matrix Estimation by Penalized Log‐Det Heuristics

期刊：Statistical Analysis and Data Mining: The ASA Data Science Journal · DOI：10.1002/sam.70115 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：Crossref 元数据

We study covariance estimation in high‐dimensional approximate factor models with a sparse residual covariance. The estimator combines a log‐det loss with nuclear‐norm and off‐diagonal penalties on a sample‐normalized covariance scale. Under light‐tailed sampling, pervasive factors and factor‐space incoherence, we establish local curvature and characterize a local fixed point of the associated threshold iteration. Its low‐rank component consistently recovers the latent rank; exact signed residual‐support recovery additionally requires a minimum‐signal condition. We derive componentwise estimation rates and sufficient conditions for positive definiteness. The analysis separates the spectral requirements for rank recovery from the entrywise requirements for residual‐support recovery. A boundary‐adaptive search identifies the two thresholds by extending an initial grid when its selected solution lies on a boundary. Simulations examine structural recovery and covariance accuracy, and compare log‐det and Frobenius losses with covariance shrinkage methods. An application to European bank supervisory indicators illustrates the resulting decomposition into latent factors and sparse residual relationships.
