构建农垦国有农用地权利体系，是落实全民所有自然资源资产所有者职责、深化农垦改革并保障国家粮食安全的基石，对防止资产流失与实现保值增值至关重要。针对农垦国有农用地长期存在的权利类型不清、权能模糊与规范缺位问题，构建国有农用地所有权—国有农用地使用权—土地承包经营权—土地经营权四层结构，为国有农用地资源资产奠定权属清晰的法律基础。研究表明：（1）明确将国有农用地使用权、承包经营权与经营权均定性为用益物权，并严格确定其权源、主体与适用范围，能够推动农地管理从行政模糊管理向精确法律关系的根本转变。（2）创新性地提出经营权的设立须满足“连续经营五年以上”和“物权式流转”双重要件，并通过对《中华人民共和国民法典》中的“出租”目的性限缩解释为物权性的“转包”，可清晰划分物权与债权的生成路径，有效促进法律体系之间的内在统一。由此不仅完成了农垦国有农用地权利体系的法律构造，也为推动国有农用地资本化与规模化经营提供了兼具法理自洽性与实践操作性的完整制度方案。

论文题名

洞庭湖区人地关系动态耦合过程及其机制

期刊：自然资源学报 · DOI：10.31497/zrzyxb.20260904 · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

在全球化与区域一体化背景下，人类活动对生态系统的作用不断增强。因此，探究洞庭湖地区人地耦合机制，对于区域实现人与自然和谐共生具有重要的理论意义。以2000—2023年洞庭湖区人类足迹及生态环境质量为基础，采用四象限图解法及最小二乘结构方程模型，分析其人地关系的时空演变、空间匹配特征及耦合过程机制。结果表明：（1）2000—2023年，人类足迹变化呈递增态势，增幅达30.34%，生态环境质量则下降8.24%；人类足迹空间分布呈现以建成区为核心、四周环绕低人类足迹的网状格局；生态环境质量较高的区域，多与森林用地重合。（2）2000—2023年，人地耦合关系表现为低人类足迹—高生态环境质量区域面积减少，低人类足迹—低生态环境质量区域面积则显著上升，人地关系出现显著变化的区域占比从67.59%增至79.15%。（3）人口规模、经济发展、生态系统结构与功能对人地耦合具有显著影响，生态系统功能对人地耦合起负向作用，生态系统结构、人口规模和经济发展发挥正向作用。研究明确了洞庭湖区开展生态修复分区管控和差异化修复策略的有效空间路径，为全国其他区域生态修复调控和人与自然和谐共生的实现提供科学依据。

川滇黔长征国家文化公园作为红军长征途中极具里程碑意义的标志性空间载体，兼具高辨识度文旅品牌价值与红色文化传承功能，是创新红旅融合发展机制的重要范例。在虚实交互场景下，亟需借助旅游空间错位深挖供需关系内核，破解区域发展失衡的困境，助推实现文化赓续传承、产业提质增效与跨区域协同发展。采用2013—2022年川滇黔长征国家文化公园A级景区和网络关注度数据，运用景区丰度指数和空间错位模型，探究市（自治州）—县（区、市）双元尺度下旅游空间错位的时空演化规律及驱动机制。研究表明：（1）在时空分布上，旅游景区丰度与网络关注度均呈“S”型上升，景区分布东密西疏，由多中心向网络化发展，形成黔东南—安顺—贵阳、遵义—泸州—宜宾两大核心；高网络关注度由离散转向集聚，形成甘孜藏族自治州、阿坝藏族羌族自治州和遵义市等集聚区。（2）在错位关系上，市（自治州）尺度以中错位为主，呈西北—东南的负—正—负分异；县（区、市）尺度以低错位、正错位为主。（3）不同尺度空间错位具有相对性与尺度敏感性，69%的市（自治州）与县（区、市）错位方向一致，31%的市（自治州）与县（区、市）则存在尺度分异。州正县负区域需提升资源整合度与形象识别度，打造红农旅融合品牌；州负县正区域则要资源联动、客源互通、区域协同，推动红色文旅与乡村振兴高质量发展。

论文题名

Spatial Restructuring of Rural Settlements in the North China Plain During the Ming–Qing Transition (1630–1680): A Multi-Temporal Gis Analysis of Historical Settlement Data

期刊：Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy · DOI：10.1007/s12061-026-10002-5 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Rural settlements form a fundamental layer of cultural heritage, yet most existing studies analyse their distribution at a single temporal cross-section, leaving the dynamics of historical restructuring underexplored. This study constructs a multi-temporal GIS framework to reconstruct rural settlement distributions in the North China Plain at three time slices (1630, 1644, and 1680) spanning the Ming to Qing dynastic transition. Using 13,961 contemporary village locations as a spatial baseline, historical presence is estimated through toponymic analysis, administrative record linkage, and a probabilistic attrition model. Spatial pattern methods, Geodetector, and multiscale geographically weighted regression are then applied to trace disruption and recovery and to identify the geographic factors associated with settlement survival. Results indicate that the settlement total declined by a net 18.1% between 1630 and 1644, with only partial recovery by 1680 leaving the total 12.5% below the late Ming baseline. Loss was spatially concentrated in the western uplands of Zhangde Fu and the southern margins of Weihui Fu. Overlap analysis with the national traditional village inventory identifies over 7,400 model-classified persistent settlements that are not currently included in the heritage protection framework and may warrant further field verification. The findings provide a replicable template for event-scale settlement research and empirical grounding for heritage inventory refinement.

论文题名

Beyond Scaling Laws: Mobility-Based Explanations of Scale-Adjusted Economic Performance in the Pearl River Delta

期刊：Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy · DOI：10.1007/s12061-026-10030-1 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Urban scaling identifies economic performance relative to the output expected from population size but does not, by itself, identify why particular places depart from that benchmark. We examine whether population mobility and network position are associated with scale-adjusted economic performance (SAEP) in China’s Pearl River Delta (PRD) during 2019–2023. Annual SAEP is the residual from the GDP–population scaling relationship. Spatial panel models relate SAEP to intercity and intracity flows, directional imbalance, and network centrality across weekdays, weekends, and holidays, with robustness and endogeneity checks. The population hierarchy remained broadly stable, with only slight flattening, while spatial differences in SAEP persisted. Some weekday and weekend flow coefficients were statistically significant in parsimonious models but weakened in full specifications and were not robust across sensitivity analyses. Network centrality added little explanatory information. The spatial autoregressive coefficient was consistently negative and significant, indicating conditional spatial contrast among neighboring units rather than causal displacement or siphoning. Reverse-direction and IV checks did not resolve causal ordering. These findings delimit the explanatory reach of aggregate mobility: flow and centrality are relational proxies and do not identify a general mechanism of SAEP variation.

论文题名

Monthly Rhythms of Intercity Travel: A Seasonal Analysis of Population Mobility Between Chinese Cities

期刊：Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy · DOI：10.1007/s12061-026-10034-x · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

With the continuous improvement of China’s high-speed transportation system and the deepening of regional integration, intercity travel has become increasingly frequent and has emerged as an important field for studying spatial linkages and regional interaction among cities. Based on AMap Migration big data, we analyze the monthly rhythms and seasonal fluctuations of intercity travel throughout 2023. By examining flow characteristics at the national, city, and city-pair scales, this study reveals the monthly rhythm of intercity travel in China. Using K-means clustering and the temporal Gini coefficient, the study investigates the spatiotemporal patterns of intercity travel. The results show that intercity travel in China exhibits clear seasonality at the national scale, with peaks concentrated mainly in January, August, and October, and troughs concentrated in February–March and November–December. At the city scale, urban rhythmic patterns can be classified into four types: highly balanced, weak-rhythm, moderate-rhythm, and strong-rhythm. Economically developed large cities generally exhibit higher travel volumes and weaker seasonal fluctuations, whereas cities in western and northeastern regions tend to have smaller intercity travel volumes and stronger seasonal variability. At the city-pair scale, intercity travel with weaker seasonality is concentrated mainly within metropolitan areas and within the same province. These different types of cities and city pairs display marked differences in spatial distribution and travel demand. This study provides valuable insight into the seasonal patterns and spatial variation of intercity travel in China and has important theoretical and practical implications for urban transportation planning and policymaking.

Geopolitics Meets Trade: How Political Proximity Shapes China’s Export Resilience

期刊：Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy · DOI：10.1007/s12061-026-10016-z · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Amid escalating global economic uncertainty and the resurgence of trade protectionism in developed economies, exports have become increasingly important as a key driver of China’s economic growth, particularly under the deepening implementation of the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI). Existing studies have mainly focused on how economic structure, geographical location, and export complexity affect export resilience, while the role of bilateral political relations in shaping national trade patterns has received relatively limited attention. To address this gap, this study constructs political proximity using Chinese Customs trade data and CEPII bilateral political relations data covering the period from 2003 to 2019. It further quantifies the impact of political proximity on China’s export resilience and conducts heterogeneity analyses across provinces and products. The results show that: (1) during the early period (2003–2012), political proximity had no significant effect on provincial export resilience but exerted a significantly negative effect on product-level export resilience; (2) during the later period (2013–2019), following the launch of the Belt and Road Initiative, political proximity significantly enhanced export resilience at both the provincial and product levels; (3) the impact of political proximity on the export resilience of provinces with different levels of technology and products varies significantly. By incorporating political factors into the analytical framework of China’s export resilience, this study provides new evidence on the role of political linkages in shaping international trade and offers valuable policy implications for developing countries seeking to strengthen export resilience under an increasingly complex and uncertain global trading environment.

Spatial Associations Between the Neighborhood Built Environment and Mental Health: Evidence from Tehran

期刊：Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy · DOI：10.1007/s12061-026-10028-9 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Built environments influence mental health, yet evidence on how different dimensions of urban form relate to specific mental health outcomes remains mixed, particularly in high-density cities. This study examined these relationships in Tehran, Iran, while accounting for spatial dependence and exploring spatial heterogeneity. Using secondary neighborhood-level data from 356 neighborhoods, five mental health outcomes—anxiety, depression, social dysfunction, somatic symptoms, and overall psychological distress—were analyzed in relation to the built environment. Ordinary Least Squares (OLS), Spatial Durbin Models (SDM), and Geographically Weighted Regression (GWR) were employed to assess global associations, spatial spillover effects, and localized spatial variation. The findings indicate that residential density emerged as the built-environment characteristic most consistently associated with poorer mental health, whereas population density showed no significant direct association with any mental health outcome. Access to daily services were associated with lower somatic symptoms and social dysfunction. Anxiety and depression were explained mainly by neighborhood socio-demographic characteristics, particularly unemployment and neighborhood age structure. Spatial spillover effects were identified only for social dysfunction, while exploratory GWR analyses indicated that neighborhood–mental health associations were predominantly global across Tehran, with only modest evidence of localized spatial variation. These findings demonstrate that different dimensions of urban density have distinct implications for mental health and that different mental health outcomes respond to different neighborhood characteristics. Overall, the results suggest that improving the quality of high-density residential environments, alongside addressing neighborhood socioeconomic inequalities, may be more effective than focusing on urban density alone when promoting mental health in high-density cities.

Village morphology identification is sensitive to analytical scale, especially where administrative or built-up boundaries aggregate multiple settlement units into a single external form. This issue is particularly relevant to traditional villages, whose internal settlement composition may differ from the morphology expressed by the overall village boundary. This study examines 55 traditional villages in Northeast China and develops a sub-village spatial unit (SVSU)-based multi-scale framework to identify village morphology and diagnose scale mismatch. Six morphology indicators were used to classify SVSU-level morphology into compact, linear, and dispersed types. Village-level morphology was derived by integrating SVSU-level types according to area composition and compared with conventional village-level identification. Morphology diversity emerged as a structural correlate of scale mismatch, while SVSU number, dominant SVSU proportion, and topographic type were not significant. SDE and MBR showed higher diagnostic importance than building agglomeration indicators in differentiating assigned SVSU types. Topographic context modified the relative diagnostic importance of morphology indicators. The framework provides spatial evidence for identifying internally heterogeneous traditional villages and supports more differentiated conservation planning.

The rapid expansion of the digital economy has generated substantial environmental costs, necessitating a reconciliation between digital growth and ecological sustainability. Green finance has emerged as a policy instrument to address this tension, yet empirical evidence remains limited. This study investigates whether and how China’s green finance pilot policy drives the green transformation of the digital economy (GTDE). Using panel data from 288 prefecture-level cities (2012–2022) and a framework integrating dynamic DML, spatial quantile DML, and causal forest simulation, we find that the policy significantly promotes GTDE, with an effect of 0.0079–0.0082 (a 6–7% improvement). Heterogeneity shows stronger effects in cities with high financial development, stringent environmental regulations, and high human capital. Counterfactual simulations predict a 6.0% GTDE gain if the policy is expanded to all non-pilot cities. These findings offer causal evidence that green finance can drive the green transformation of the digital economy.

Metro Station Placement, Land-Value Gradients, and Accessibility Inequality: A Case Study of Wuhan, China

期刊：Applied Spatial Analysis and Policy · DOI：10.1007/s12061-026-10029-8 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Metro systems influence both land markets and the spatial distribution of accessibility, but these two outcome dimensions do not necessarily move together. This study examines metro station placement in Wuhan from two observable perspectives: parcel-level land-value gradients around metro stations and the distribution of community-level accessibility changes. Using 2,711 land transfers during 2012–2023, pre-construction vacant-land mapping around stations, and a community-level accessibility assessment centered on Metro Line 4, we combine descriptive spatial analysis, hedonic price models, a PSM-DID analysis, and distributional accessibility indicators. The results show that several later metro lines were spatially associated with substantial undeveloped land prior to construction. The hedonic models reveal a negative price-distance gradient in the full Wuhan sample. Within the Line 4 corridor, this gradient remains negative and significant in corridor-wide specifications but becomes statistically insignificant after district fixed effects are introduced, indicating sensitivity to broader spatial heterogeneity. The Line 4-specific PSM-DID estimates are consistently positive across nearest-neighbor, kernel, and caliper matching, indicating a positive post-opening land-price differential within the 1,000-m station area. The accessibility analysis further shows that average potential accessibility improved after Line 4 opened, although the gains remained unevenly distributed across communities. Additional hedonic specifications using the community-level accessibility score suggest that local station proximity and broader network accessibility capture related but distinct dimensions of metro-related land-market outcomes. Overall, the evidence points to a clear land-value gradient around stations and uneven accessibility gains from Line 4.

Large language models (LLMs) excel at various natural language processing tasks but still suffer from prevalent shortcomings such as factual hallucination, constrained reasoning capabilities, and unexplainable decision-making processes. Knowledge graphs (KGs), with their structured knowledge representation and robust logical reasoning power, provide an effective pathway to address these limitations. Moving beyond existing surveys that primarily catalog technical methods, this study constructs a hierarchical "root-phenomenon-consequence" theoretical framework grounded in the cognitive science foundations of neuro-symbolic integration. This framework identifies the representation gap, synergy bottleneck, and paradigm conflict as the three fundamental challenges hindering the deep integration of KGs and LLMs. A five-dimensional taxonomy of technical approaches is proposed, encompassing prompt engineering, retrieval augmented generation, model fine-tuning, large reasoning model collaboration, and knowledge agents. The evolutionary logic of this taxonomy, which progresses from external guidance to autonomous cognition, is elucidated. At the application level, innovative practices of KG-LLM collaborative frameworks are highlighted in vertical domains such as health care, law, and scientific research. Finally, current challenges in neuro-symbolic alignment and knowledge timeliness are summarized, along with perspectives on future directions, including lightweight reasoning engines and high-trust agent systems.

Counterfactual reasoning and causal intervention for multi-modal rumor detection

期刊：Knowledge and Information Systems · DOI：10.1007/s10115-026-02875-2 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Traditional rumor detection methods that rely on statistical correlations struggle to effectively distinguish between spurious associations and genuine causal relationships, leading to limited reliability under local perturbations and dataset-specific biases. To address this challenge, a causal reasoning-based general framework for multi-modal rumor detection named CCMRD is presented. First, by integrating two complementary mechanisms—feature-level textual counterfactual intervention and image dot-product causal intervention—CCMRD mitigates modality-specific spurious correlations. The textual branch reduces the model’s reliance on observable lexical shortcut cues, whereas the image branch approximates adjustment for latent visual-context factors. Second, CCMRD further incorporates social propagation structural features modeled using graph neural networks, which employ a multihead collaborative attention mechanism to achieve deep alignment and semantic enhancement of cross-modal features. Finally, extensive experiments are conducted on the WEIBO and PHEME datasets. The experimental results show that compared with those of existing state-of-the-art methods, CCMRD achieves accuracy and F1 scores of 92.91% and 92.82%, respectively, on the WEIBO dataset and 91.11% and 91.12%, respectively, on the PHEME datasets, representing improvements of approximately 2 percentage points. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed counterfactual and intervention mechanisms for in-domain multi-modal rumor detection, providing methodological support for trustworthy multi-modal information understanding systems.

论文题名

Class probability and polycentric fuzzy least squares twin support vector machine for imbalanced data

期刊：Knowledge and Information Systems · DOI：10.1007/s10115-026-02862-7 · 语言：en · 摘要来源：出版方摘要

Twin support vector machine (TSVM) is a widely recognized model for classification problems; however, it struggles to handle noise and outliers effectively and may yields biased results on imbalanced datasets. To resolve these issues, we develop a novel class probability and polycentric fuzzy least squares twin support vector machine (CPLTSVM) for imbalanced datasets. The proposed model assigns fuzzy membership weights based on the proximity of samples to their nearest class centers, thereby incorporating the global structural information of the dataset. Furthermore, we integrate the proposed membership function with the least squares twin support vector machine to lower the computational cost of the model. Moreover, the k-nearest-neighbor technique is employed to estimate class probabilities, thereby reducing the influence of noisy samples. To evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed model, experiments are conducted on twenty benchmark imbalanced datasets, and the results are compared with those of several state-of-the-art techniques. The performance of the model using both linear and nonlinear kernels is evaluated using AUC, G-mean, and F-measure, followed by comprehensive statistical analyses. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed model consistently surpasses the existing techniques, exhibiting an astounding average AUC value of 89.71% and 94.87% for the linear and nonlinear kernels, respectively, highlighting its superior generalization performance. Furthermore, the practical utility of the proposed model is validated through the medical diagnosis of breast cancer using the BreakHis dataset, where it achieves superior performance compared to the baseline methods, thereby highlighting its effectiveness for real-world classification tasks.
